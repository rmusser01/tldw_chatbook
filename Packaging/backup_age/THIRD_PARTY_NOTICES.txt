The backup-age helper's `go version -m` inventory records these statically linked
non-standard modules:

- filippo.io/age v1.3.2
- filippo.io/hpke v0.4.0
- golang.org/x/crypto v0.55.0

The helper also contains the Go 1.26.2 standard library/runtime. The native wheel
includes the exact upstream license files as LICENSE.age.txt, LICENSE.hpke.txt,
LICENSE.x-crypto.txt, and LICENSE.go.txt, plus the Go project's PATENTS grant as
PATENTS.go.txt. The macOS system libraries named by the Mach-O load commands are
provided by the operating system and are not redistributed in the wheel.

Qualification-only CLI/test modules in go.mod are not linked into the helper and are
not binary-wheel notice owners. Release owners must repeat `go version -m`, the
non-standard dependency inventory, and applicable license/patent inspection whenever
a source pin or Go toolchain changes.
