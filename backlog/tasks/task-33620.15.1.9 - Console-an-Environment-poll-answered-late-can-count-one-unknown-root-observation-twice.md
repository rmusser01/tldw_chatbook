## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
TASK-33620.15.1.6 moved the Environment panel's root read onto a worker. The panel then says it cannot determine the workspace after fewer real ticks than _MAX_UNKNOWN_TICKS intends. It only matters while nothing has ever landed, and needs a root read slower than 10 s (measured worker reads peaked at 433 ms), so it is minor; raised in the TASK-33620.15.1.6 review.
