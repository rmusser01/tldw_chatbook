## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
The rider's refresh premise is false at fc9f3ec375 (Phase 9 Wave 3 head). Round-1 review found that AC#1 still failed on the rail path, and that is fixed in the rail-focus footer.

- No refresh is missing. When Chat settings closes, `ChatScreen.on_screen_resume` re-registers the Console footer twice: directly, and through `_sync_console_transcript_guidance` → `_sync_console_setup_modal` → `_apply_console_setup_block`. The control-bar sync re-registers it again. Each registration reads the live focus (`_console_rail_focus_active`).
- The rail footer the entry-points test once read after Esc matched the real focus. Focus had stayed on the rail's Model toggle after the rail's Change action, not after Chat settings. TASK-33007 found this in 3bc3d9c33b and rewrote `_close_chat_settings` to press Esc, so AC#2 already held.
- But that rail footer dropped Alt+A and Alt+C at 211x44. So AC#1 failed when Chat settings was opened from a rail control and closed by Esc. TASK-32322 prepends "Esc composer · F6 panes", and the footer keeps only a prefix of its hints, so it read `Esc composer · F6 panes | Ctrl+Shift+F focus | F6 next pane | F1 help | Alt+M switch model | Ctrl+O chat settings | Ctrl+K switch session | Ctrl+T new tab | Alt+I inspect | Ctrl+P · Ctrl+Q`. This happened with or without a modal, and it undid TASK-33004.7's "keep Alt+I, Alt+A and Alt+C at 211x44" whenever focus was in a rail.
- The fix is in `_register_console_footer_shortcuts` (chat_screen.py), which, while focus is in a rail, takes its hints from `UI/Console_Modules/left_rail.py::rail_focus_shortcuts` (moved there in the fix-round review). It adds no method.
  - Under the rail prefix, two of the Console's own hints are left out: "F6 next pane", and a collapsed composer's "Esc show composer". The prefix already names both keys, and the app cluster still lists F6.
  - Ctrl+K and Ctrl+T move behind the rail's Alt+I, Alt+A and Alt+C.
  - The setup-blocked variant keeps its F6-unavailable entry, and the composer footer is unchanged.
- The footers at 211x44:
  - Rail: `Esc composer · F6 panes | Ctrl+Shift+F focus | F1 help | Alt+M switch model | Ctrl+O chat settings | Alt+I inspect | Alt+A approval | Alt+C context rail | Ctrl+K switch session | F6 · Ctrl+P · Ctrl+Q`.
  - Inspect rail: `Esc composer · F6 panes | n/p Sections | Ctrl+Shift+F focus | F1 help | Alt+M switch model | Ctrl+O chat settings | Alt+I inspect | Alt+A approval | Alt+C context rail | F6 · Ctrl+P · Ctrl+Q`, with the composer expanded or collapsed.
  - Ctrl+T, and Ctrl+K in the Inspect rail, appear on wider terminals.

Test: `Tests/UI/test_console_switch_model_entry_points.py::test_chat_settings_closed_by_esc_leaves_the_focused_widgets_footer`.
- It opens Chat settings from the rail's Model toggle, then from the composer, and closes each by Esc. It asserts that focus went back to where it was opened, and that the footer is that widget's: the rail's exits lead for the toggle and not for the composer. On both paths the footer must keep Alt+A and Alt+C.
- It then checks the Inspect rail's footer with the composer expanded and collapsed: it shows n/p, names Esc once, and keeps Alt+A and Alt+C.

- With both resume-time re-registrations removed (round 0), it times out on the covered footer. Removing only one still passes, because the setup-block path re-registers.
