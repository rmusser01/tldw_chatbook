## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The Trash view's state build, sync, focus, restore and permanent-delete bodies still live on LibraryScreen and are reached through six controller callbacks, while the Trash search, filter and paging handlers already live on the controller. Moving them gives the Trash surface one home.

Source: architecture review 2026-10-25, `Docs/QA/architecture-review-2026-10-25/report.md` §F2; slice 4 of 8 in the TASK-36952.3 split plan, measured at `08b3c9d337`.
