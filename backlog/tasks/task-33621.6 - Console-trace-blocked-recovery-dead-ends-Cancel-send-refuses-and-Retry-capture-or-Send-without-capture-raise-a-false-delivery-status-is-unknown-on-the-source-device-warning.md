## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
**Impact (P1).** This was reproduced twice after a first call got HTTP 429 or 500 and the retry's trace reservation then failed before dispatch (reservation_status 'unknown'). It happens on the 'Trace capture blocked' card:
- **Cancel send never cancels.** The card's status changes to 'Trace reservation status is unavailable.' and the card stays, even after repeated clicks. It is the one escape hatch, and it fails.
- **Send without capture and Retry capture make no provider call.** There is no POST and no send-stage log line. Each shows 'Working…' for about 1 s and is then replaced by 'Response delivery status is unknown on the source device. / Retry anyway may send a duplicate request because delivery status is unknown. [Retry anyway] [Discard]'. That is false: the only POST was the rejected 429/500, the retry never left the app, and the session is local. The 'source device' sentence is remote-handoff copy (console_chat_models.py:695).
- **Retry anyway works but gives no acknowledgement.** Its row stays byte-identical for 4.2 s until the reply arrives.
- **Header disagrees.** The composer, rail and Inspect handle say blocked while the header chip says 'Ready'.

Across three panels, the app makes three different claims about the same send, and users are pushed toward Discard.

**Prior art:** KNOWN_PARTIAL: TASK-25823 (Done; remote-handoff copy during local sends, same family), TASK-31976 (Done), TASK-25814 (Done), PR #2882 (TASK-32951, TASK-32953). The verifier noted that a 'provider_entry never entered' rule would not catch this case. Delivery state has to come from the failed call's definitive HTTP outcome together with the fact that the retry never crossed dispatch.

**Suggested direction:**
- Record 'provider rejected' and 'not dispatched' outcomes instead of 'unknown'.
- Make Cancel unconditional from the user's side: mark an unknown reservation abandoned locally, settle it later, and restore the draft.
- Make Retry capture re-run capture.
- Split the dispatch-recovery copy into local and remote variants.
- Add an immediate 'Retrying…' busy state.
- Derive the header chip from run state.

The row's evidence files, uxrev/evidence/L3/59-cancel-send-500.txt, 08-send-without-capture-result.txt, 10-retry-anyway-working.txt and J2/103-retry-capture.png, were not copied into qa/evidence. ### Lessons (kept here; lessons-live-verification.md is another session's open work)

Moved verbatim from this branch's addition to backlog/docs/lessons-live-verification.md (headings demoted to stay inside these notes).

#### A held write lock gives an *unknown* trace reservation, never a refused one: the refused-retry path has no live injection (TASK-33621.6, 2026-11-21)

**Incident.** The trace card's Retry had been broken on dev since #3346. Its mounted test was red in 3 of 3 runs, and the cause was a mix-in binding refused *inside* the retry's reservation transaction. Reproducing it live needs that retry's reservation to roll back as a refusal (`not_established`), and neither injection the review recipe offers does that. A `BEGIN EXCLUSIVE` held across the 429's automatic retry makes the retry's `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` time out (`sqlite_code=5`). That outcome is *unknown*, and the card's Retry then says "could not be confirmed" without ever reaching the code that broke. The proxy's `cut` mode (drop mid-stream) gave three traced in-place retries and a Failed row, with no card at all. The live round proved the neighbouring paths, and the mounted ChatScreen test carried the regression.

**What to do.** Before planning a live repro of a trace-card path, read which reservation status it needs. "Unknown" comes from a lock (lessons above). "Refused" needs a `ValueError` raised inside the reservation transaction, which only a code-level fault produces. Say in the evidence which branch the live round exercised. Do not count a held-lock round as a check of the refused path.

#### A `ready` launcher profile's first launch can open "Check model lists online?" and swallow the first keystrokes (TASK-33621.70, 2026-11-21)

**Incident.** On origin/dev dc371f0842, a fresh `ready` profile opened the model-list consent modal over the Console even though the recipe says it stays off outside `fresh` mode. Text sent with `send-keys -l` went to the modal, and the composer stayed at "Esc to type". The driver waited for a POST that never came, then SIGKILLed an app with nothing in flight, and the whole round had to be relaunched. A separate trap in the same session: `shot.py` names its files with `Path.with_suffix`, so an output name ending in "-0.4s" was written as "-0.txt".

**What to do.** After every fresh launch, capture the pane and dismiss any modal (find "Don't check" by character column; `grep -b` gives byte offsets, and box-drawing characters are 3 bytes). Confirm the composer shows the typed text before pressing Enter. Keep dots out of shot names.
