## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Keep ephemeral generated-video storage bounded throughout a long-running app session without reapplying startup cleanup. When one generated video is larger than the configured cap, preserve user control by offering an explicit managed-store exception, external save, or discard instead of silently losing media.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 After each successful ordinary video save, the active store is at or below the configured total size cap; the only permitted over-cap state is one explicitly approved oversized video as the store's sole file.
- [ ] #2 Ordinary saves evict oldest live videos first, their existing cards resolve as expired, and the newly saved video remains ready.
- [ ] #3 A video larger than the entire cap is not silently stored or discarded: initial generation and Regenerate both offer Keep here and evict all, Save to disk and open externally, or Discard.
- [ ] #4 Keep here removes all other managed videos before appending the new card; Save to disk uses an `.mp4`-default file picker with no-clobber commit and confirmed replacement, then opens externally and appends no card; Discard and picker cancellation append no card.
- [ ] #5 Post-save capacity enforcement never reapplies session or TTL startup cleanup, while the approved sole oversized file remains subject to normal session/TTL removal and is evicted by a later ordinary save.
- [ ] #6 Concurrent saves from threads or app processes sharing one store root and filesystem failures cannot report false cap compliance, expose a partial managed file, destroy existing videos on publication failure, or lose the generated payload before the user chooses another outcome; lock contention times out without hanging startup or generation.
- [ ] #7 Capacity inventory and eviction never follow symlinked directories, symlinked files, junctions, or reparse points outside the managed root, including during ordinary saves, startup capacity, and evict-all.
- [ ] #8 Focused mutation tests protect the save-time capacity hook, oldest-first order, in-process and root-scoped interprocess locks, non-following deletion, startup-policy separation, no-clobber/overwrite handling, and staged-artifact lifetime across screen teardown.
<!-- AC:END -->
