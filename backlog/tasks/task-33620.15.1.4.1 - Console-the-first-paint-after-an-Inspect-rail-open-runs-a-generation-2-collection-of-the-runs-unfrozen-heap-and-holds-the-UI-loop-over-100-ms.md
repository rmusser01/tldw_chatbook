## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found while measuring TASK-33620.15.1.4 live on origin/dev 3be493413a (isolated profiles, Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 through the review proxy, 235x52, the Inspect rail opened with Alt+I 2.5 s into a reply, host load 5-16). Once the edge focus cue stopped restyling the whole rail, the longest UI-loop callback at a rail open is the first Textual screen update after it. Over 21 opens it held the loop a median 110 ms (max 295 ms), 100 ms or more in 14 of 21. Its CPU was a median 101 ms: an automatic generation-2 collection took a median 29 ms of it (0-61 ms, 0.6-8.9k objects collected) and Textual's reflow and paint of the opened rail and the narrowed transcript took the rest (non-GC CPU median 58 ms, max 81 ms). The same shape, a screen update with a generation-2 pass inside, holds most callbacks of 100 ms or more in the 3 s after a run completes and at a rail close. In a paired round at load 15-30 (12 opens, closes and completions per arm, TASK-33620.15.1.4's fix applied), the open's first paint was 100 ms or more in 10 of 12 opens, screen updates were 14 of the 17 callbacks of 100 ms or more at a rail close, and 11 at completions. The idle heap re-freeze 2.3-2.9 s after a completion (IdleHeapRefreezer, a deliberate full collection of the unfrozen heap under ADR-198's amendment) took 102-133 ms with 76-103 ms of CPU in 7 of 24 completions.

The collection lands in that paint because the paint is the largest allocation burst while the unfrozen heap still holds what the run allocated since the last idle re-freeze, which waits for the run to settle. Closing the rail also freed about 89k frozen objects by reference count (permanent generation 848k to 759k), and the next open built them again. Evidence: the probe files p14pb-B*.jsonl and p14pt-T*.jsonl with p14gc.py and p14first.py (local-only: kept in the measuring session's private probe kit, not in the repository. TASK-33620.15.1.4's notes describe the probe and the set-up, so re-measure rather than look for these files).
