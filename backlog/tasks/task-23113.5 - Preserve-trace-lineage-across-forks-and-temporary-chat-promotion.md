## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Keep prior trace history coherently visible after forks and require temporary chats to become durable before Capture On can dispatch, without duplicating inherited trace payloads.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Forked conversations inherit reachable prior trace history by shared ownership or references and do not duplicate inherited artifacts, surfaces, events, or calls.
- [ ] Each fork records its own subsequent semantic revisions and call chronology while the viewer presents the combined history coherently.
- [ ] Editing or regenerating either branch after a fork does not rewrite the other branch or historical trace records.
- [ ] Capture On in a temporary chat pauses before dispatch and offers Save & Send or explicit Capture Off; Save & Send promotes the chat and reserves the call atomically enough to prevent an untraced dispatch.
- [ ] Promotion or fork persistence failure leaves the source conversation and its trace ownership intact and no provider request is sent.
- [ ] ADR required: yes; this task implements the ownership and conversation-lifecycle policy in ADR-097.
