## Implementation Notes

Conversation-history search admits its selected root through directory probes and segment loading; canonical readers consume that root, custom readers retain their one-argument contract, and absent roots never reselect. No permission decision is cached. ADR-126 and ADR-230 were amended before implementation; Docs/Architecture/agent-runtime.md describes the final ownership.

Causal native controls prove two initial scopes become one while actual restored-root refusal, reader/worker lifetime and passive source qualification remain enforced.
