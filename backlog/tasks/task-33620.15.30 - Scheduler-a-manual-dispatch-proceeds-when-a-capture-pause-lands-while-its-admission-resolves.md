## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found reviewing TASK-33620.15.24. Since that task, SchedulerLoop._admitted checks the maintenance fence on the UI loop and then resolves storage admission on a worker. A capture pause (_maintenance_close_admission) that lands between the two no longer refuses a manual dispatch (dispatch_reminder or run_reminder_now called outside run()): it proceeds under a closed fence. The maintenance drain still waits for it, so a capture stays safe; before, the fence check and the admission were one synchronous step, so this window did not exist. run()'s own ticks already skip in this case (TASK-33620.15.24 review follow-up).
