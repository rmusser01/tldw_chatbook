## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
TASK-33003.6 moved the grid lines and control edges of the model-configuration surfaces onto `$tldw-boundary`, which is pinned at 3:1. Some lines that every screen shares were outside that scope. The cubic review of PR #2937 measured them below the WCAG 1.4.11 3:1 floor for component boundaries, in textual-dark at 211x44, on the Phase 3 branch:
- the full-width separator under the top tab bar: (62,62,62) on (18,18,18), 1.75:1;
- the active top tab's outline: (1,120,212) on (0,101,190), 1.29:1;
- the app-wide Collapsible frame, `border: round $surface-lighten-1` in components/_widgets.tcss: (45,45,45) on (30,30,30), 1.21:1. It shows in Settings (for example "▶ Where changes happen" and Providers & Models' "Generation defaults");
- the Console rail's section rules: (62,62,62) on (30,30,30), 1.56:1.

Library's pane frames are already covered by TASK-33003.15 and are not in scope here.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
The top tab-bar separator, the active top tab's outline, the shared Collapsible frame and the Console rail's section rules now read at 3:1 on every measurable theme.

**Where the two unplaced lines live.**
- The separator is `MainNavigationBar`'s own `border-bottom` (UI/Navigation/main_navigation.py, `BUNDLED_CSS`), painted on the bar's `background: $background`.
- The outline is not the nav bar's rule. The app tier's `.is-active { border: round $ds-action-focus }` (components/_agentic_terminal.tcss) paints it, on the `$primary-darken-1` fill that the bar's widget-tier `.nav-button.is-active` rule sets. Tier beats specificity in Textual, so the bar's own `border: solid $primary` never painted.

**Fixes.**
- Collapsible frame (components/_widgets.tcss): `border: round $ds-grid-line`, on the rule's own `$surface`. Same bare-type rule, one value changed.
- Console rail section rules (features/_console.tcss): `border-top: solid $ds-column-line`, which TASK-33003.15 pointed at `$tldw-boundary`. No rule changed here; this rider adds their gate.
- Tab-bar separator: the bar now paints `$surface`, the fill its tabs already rest on, and the separator is `$ds-grid-line`. The clip-ghost rules paint the same fill, in the widget tier and in their app-tier copy (components/_navigation.tcss), so a ghosted tab still vanishes into the bar.
- Active tab outline: a third guard variable, `tldw-active-edge` (themes.py `ACTIVE_EDGE_VARIABLE`). `ensure_readable_text_hues` starts from the theme's `primary` and blends toward white or black, whichever first reaches 3:1 on `primary-darken-1`; a hand-set entry that already holds is kept. It rides `GUARD_VARIABLES`, so the unmeasurable-theme fallback, `ThemeVariableDefaultsMixin` and the theme editor's pinned-key filter cover it. A new app-tier rule `.nav-button.is-active, .nav-button.is-active:focus` sets that outline and restates the fill.

- Ruling: move the bar onto `$surface` instead of widening the boundary guard to `$background`. Why: `$tldw-boundary` fails 3:1 on `$background` on 6 shipped themes (litestep 1.32:1), and re-pinning it there would darken every grid line and field edge on those themes (litestep #727270 to #393938) to fix one line. Cost if wrong: the bar's empty tail and its separator row paint `$surface` where they painted `$background`; three tokens to put back.
- Ruling: the active tab keeps its own fill hovered and focused. Why: the app tier's `Button:hover` and `Button:focus` fills replaced it, and no single outline colour reads at 3:1 on all three. With the new outline on the old focus fill, textual-light measured 1.66:1 (the old one read 4.21:1 there) and pastel_dreams 2.70:1. The widget's own `.nav-button.is-active:focus` rule already said "keep the fill, bold underline"; a variant Button does the same. Cost if wrong: a keyboard-focused active tab shows focus by underline alone, where it also changed fill.
- Ruling: a new guard variable, not a generated one. Why: no variable Textual generates clears 3:1 on `primary-darken-1` on all 89 themes; the best, `text`, fails on 3. Cost if wrong: one more name every theme carries.

**Gate (AC#1)**, Tests/UI/test_theme_contrast.py:
- `test_app_chrome_lines_clear_non_text_contrast`: 5 lines (the four above; the rail has two rules) by 89 measurable themes. It reads each line's colour token from its rule and the fill from that rule's `background`, resolves both through `_variables.tcss`, and asserts 3:1.
- `test_app_chrome_lines_paint_at_non_text_contrast`: mounts the nav bar, a Collapsible and a rail section header under the production stylesheet (bundle and split sheets) on textual-dark, textual-light and pastel_dreams, reads each line's cells from the frame, and measures the active tab at rest, hovered, hovered and focused, and focused.
- `_GUARD_PROBE_CSS` now references `$tldw-active-edge`, so the parse test for all 91 registered themes and the bare-`Theme()` fallback test cover the new name.
- Red before the fix: 358 failed. 89 on the Collapsible frame, 85 on the separator, 89 on the missing active-tab rule, 92 on the undefined variable, 3 rendered. The two rail rules were already green after TASK-33003.15; with its token put back they fail 178 of 178 (textual-dark 1.56:1).
- Negative controls on the rendered test: with the new rule removed from the bundle it fails on all three themes (textual-dark #0178D4 on #0065BE, 1.29:1); with only its `:focus` selector removed, or only its `background`, it fails "hovered and focused" on textual-light and pastel_dreams.
