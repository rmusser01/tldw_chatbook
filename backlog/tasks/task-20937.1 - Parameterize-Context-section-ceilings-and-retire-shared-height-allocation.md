## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Make Context sections honor their approved 15/20/35 natural-height ceilings, preserve separate local hints, and use ordinary Context outer scrolling without no-room reprioritization while Inspector stays fixed at 20.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [x] #1 The shared bounded-section primitive accepts validated per-instance Context ceilings of 15, 20, or 35 rows, normalizes allocations against that instance ceiling, and keeps Inspector's existing 20-row construction and behavior unchanged.
- [x] #2 Sessions, Model, Agent, and Details use 15; Workspaces and Conversations use 20; Character uses 35; exact natural/ceiling/overflow cases prove 14/15/16, 19/20/21, and 34/35/36 physical-row transitions with a separate one-row local hint.
- [x] #3 Context retires shared sibling-height allocation, active reprioritization, `· no room`, and `[>]`; every open section receives its natural/capped body and the outer Context body becomes the normal reachability owner when complete sections exceed its viewport.
- [x] #4 Outer and local hint predicates remain counterfactual and non-sticky; ordinary mutation preserves local and outer offsets, shrink clamps them, and focus/wheel handoff plus open preferences remain unchanged.
- [x] #5 Production-shaped focused tests prove multiple all-open sections retain their own ceilings at tall and short heights, every header/body remains reachable, and no Context change regresses Inspector's 20/21 behavior.
