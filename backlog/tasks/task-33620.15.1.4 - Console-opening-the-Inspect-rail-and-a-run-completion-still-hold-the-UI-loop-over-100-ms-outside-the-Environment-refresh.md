## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
TASK-33620.15.1.3 removed the Environment panel's turn-configuration build from the UI loop. The same live rounds (isolated profiles, Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5, 235x52, load 4.6-10) still logged 11-14 UI-loop callbacks of 100 ms or more per round, up to 666 ms. They were MessagePump and run_app tasks, not the Environment refresh, and are not yet attributed. Input waits behind each one.

One lead, not measured: the Inspect rail's next-send preview (UI/Console_Modules/inspector_data.py) calls controller.resolve_turn_execution_context(session_id) once in its factory and again in its staleness check. That is the same full turn-configuration build that cost the Environment panel 0.6-1.4 s per rail open.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
**Summary.** Measured first. The largest app-level cost at a rail open and close was the Console's edge focus cue: `sync_console_focus_paint` set `console-edge-region-focused` with a plain `set_class`, so each focus move into or out of a rail restyled that rail's whole subtree. It now restyles only the rail and its own collapse/open control, as the transcript cue already does. Every other callback of 100 ms or more at a rail open, a rail close and a run completion is attributed below and filed.

**Set-up.** Real app, isolated profiles, null keyring, Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 through the review proxy, 235x52, `caffeinate` held. Each launch made four sends ("write a 160-word paragraph about X"), opened the Inspect rail with Alt+I 2.5 s after Enter, and closed it 12 s later. The probe was pf2probe with a run-completion marker (`complete_definitive_run`) and exact wall/CPU timers on the suspect seams (identity tables checked first). Base was origin/dev 3be493413a. Both arms ran from package-only exports (`git archive`) after the host's disk filled mid-round. Evidence is local-only: the probe, the driver (p14drive.py, p14pair.sh), the stdlib analysis scripts and the probe files p14pb-B*, p14pt-T*, p14b-Q* and p14f-Q* sit in the measuring session's private probe kit, not in the repository, so whoever picks up a follow-up re-measures rather than looks for them. The probe is a sitecustomize hook: it records every UI-loop callback of 2 ms or more (asyncio `Handle._run`) with its wall time, thread CPU and GC time, every generation-2 collection, and a 4 ms main-thread stack sample (`sys._current_frames`) while a callback has run 4 ms or more; the exact timers wrap the suspect functions at the binding their callers use.

**What the task's numbers were.** At load 5-8 each base launch had 12-13 callbacks of 100 ms or more. 7-10 of them ran before the first send: the compose (run_app, 575-705 ms) and the mounts (MessagePump). Only 1-3 fell at a rail open or a completion. The task's lead, the Next Send preview building the turn configuration twice, is not on the rail-open path: `build_next_send_factories` serves the Conversation Inspector modal (cost chip, Ctrl+Shift+P).

**Attribution (base, load 5-16, 21 opens, 12 completions).**
- Rail open:
  - The first Textual screen update was a median 110 ms wall and 101 ms CPU, 100 ms or more in 14/21. A generation-2 pass took a median 29 ms of it; Textual reflow and paint took the non-GC CPU (median 58 ms).
  - `sync_console_focus_paint` (DescendantFocus handler) took a median 47 ms per open, up to 213 ms.
  - The Alt+I callback (`_set_console_rail_preference`) was mostly the Environment root's consent reads (TASK-33620.15.1.6), up to 313 ms wall on 28 ms CPU.
- Rail close: the focus paint took a median 62 ms, up to 293 ms.
- Completion:
  - Screen updates with generation-2 passes.
  - The poll tick's `_sync_console_rail_and_controls`: 37-159 ms per completion; its full rail-state build is about 29 ms.
  - The coalesced Agent-fleet sync (up to 182 ms).
  - The idle re-freeze.
  - `ConsoleRuntime._finish_custodied_turn` -> `recompute_console_attention`: up to 101 ms wall on 16 ms CPU.

**Fix.** `UI/Console_Modules/frame.py`: `_paint_edge_region_focus` sets the class with `update=False` and restyles only the region and its control. It sets the control's bold/underline as before. After review, the edge cue and the transcript cue share one helper, `_set_class_restyling_only`, so a fourth instance has a place to go.
