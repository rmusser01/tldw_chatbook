Found live on dev 3be493413a while measuring TASK-33620.15.24 (Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5, 160x45, isolated profile, 2026-10-27). When the next characters are typed into the composer straight after Enter (before the send is durably accepted), the turn ends with RecoveryRequired (log: 'Console runtime turn ended with exception_type=RecoveryRequired'), no provider request is made, the transcript stays 'No messages yet', and the composer keeps the unsent text with the new typing appended to it. Evidence: eager-typing control p24c3 (5 sends, 1 reached the provider, 3 RecoveryRequired); tick-aligned rounds p24m3/p24m4 (10/10 sends refused); controls with a 3 s pause after Enter p24c1 (4/4 reached the provider) and a 12 s pause before Enter p24c2 (3/3). Likely refusal: console_received_dispatch.require_received_source -> store.session_inputs_are_current(record.received_intent.inputs) raising RecoveryRequired('console_snapshot_owner_changed') once the draft changes, but this was not traced.
