## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
`config.py` creates three database singletons lazily (`get_chachanotes_db_lazy`, `get_prompts_db_lazy`, `get_media_db_lazy` at `:11873`, `:11908` and `:11937`). That makes the config module a global service locator for the app, `app_feature_glue`, the CCP character handler, `Character_Chat_Lib`, the document expansion tool, `Utils/doctor.py` and `MCP/local_runtime_delegate.py`. Its `initialize_all_databases()` has no production caller. Under the 2026-10-25 services decision, every entry point gets its databases from the services bundle or its shared factory (TASK-36947.2).

Source: architecture review 2026-10-25, `Docs/QA/architecture-review-2026-10-25/report.md` §F9; claim-by-claim verification in `Docs/QA/architecture-review-2026-10-25/verification.md` (origin/dev 7a5e2513c5).
