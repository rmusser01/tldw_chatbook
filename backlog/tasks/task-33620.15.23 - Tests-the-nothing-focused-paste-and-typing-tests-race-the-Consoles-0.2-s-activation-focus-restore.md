## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Tests/UI/test_console_composer_run_controls.py::test_typing_with_nothing_focused_focuses_the_draft_first and its twin test_paste_with_nothing_focused_lands_in_a_focused_draft fail on origin/dev 5b5920b8e2 in most runs (typing: 5 of 6 during TASK-33620.15.8's gates; on 2026-10-23 both failed on dev and on that branch alike) with focus back on the composer after console.set_focus(None). Cause, found with a focus probe in TASK-33620.15.8: Console activation schedules _restore_console_workbench_focus twice, after refresh and on a 0.2 s timer (ChatScreen, next to _sync_native_console_chat_ui). When the timer fires after a test's set_focus(None), the restore focuses the composer bar again (the probe saw it about 30 ms after the blur), and the tests' assert app.focused is None fails. Neither test is on the UI PR lane, so this does not block PRs, but it hides whether the keyboard and paste behaviour they pin still holds.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
**2026-10-27 (branch fix/task-33620.15.17-keys-before-first-focus).**

Re-measured first: the runs this task was filed from predate the wait that TASK-33620.15.7 added (a19334f0e6, dated 2026-10-20). It reached dev only on 2026-10-26, with the merge of PR #3211 (c398d3a4d0), and 5b5920b8e2 does not contain it. On dev a2b7104d1e, before any change, both tests passed 8 of 8 serial plain runs at load averages of 20-40.

That wait was still not the condition the tests need. It waited for the mount's 0.2 s restore timer only, and returned at once when the attach reconcile had not created the timer yet. The activation schedules three restores, all from the reconcile: after refresh and on the 0.2 s timer when the view starts, and after refresh again when the reconcile replays the screen's resume.

The first replacement, reviewed at f7b8397e6d, waited for the reconcile to finish, then one round trip through the screen's queue, then until no restore was left on the screen's callback list or on a live timer. It was recorded here as passing 10 of 10 runs. That did not hold: the independent review measured 3 failures in 29 plain runs of the pair, with the same symptom (`ConsoleComposerBar` focused again right after `set_focus(None)`). The task went back to In Progress.

**Review fix (2026-10-27): the wait could not see the timer.** Textual's `set_timer` stores `partial(self.call_next, callback)`, so `timer._callback == restore` was never true. TASK-33620.15.7's wait had the same blind spot. A probe that logged every restore and focus change over 42 runs found 8 refocus failures. Every one came from the timer's restore, run through `_flush_next_callbacks`, and they were exactly the 8 runs where the wait returned with 2 of the 3 scheduled restores run. In all 34 passing runs, all 3 had run first.
- `_activation_restore_ledger` is installed before the host runs. It wraps ChatScreen's `call_after_refresh` and `set_timer`, records each scheduled `_restore_console_workbench_focus` as it is scheduled, and marks the entry once that restore has returned. That covers a restore wherever it waits: an `InvokeLater` message, the screen's callback list, a batch already copied off that list, a timer, or the timer's `call_next` hop.
- `_after_activation_focus_restores(console, pilot, restores)` waits, with no fixed sleep, until the attach reconcile has finished (so nothing more will be scheduled) and every recorded restore has run. It then makes one round trip through the app's queue (`pump_probe.pump_runs(console.app)`); a restore's `focus()` lands through `App.call_later` ahead of that round trip.

Evidence (plain command; every mutation applied with Edit, then restored):
- The wait itself, red then green. A temporary probe asserted that no recorded restore was still unrun when the wait returned. The old wait failed 1 of 6 runs as it was. With the activation timer lengthened from 0.2 s to 0.6 s, it failed 6 of 6, and each time the unrun restore was the timer's. The new wait passed 6 of 6 under that mutation, and so did the two tests.
- AC #2, repeated with the new wait. With the key path's nothing-focused focus removed, the typing test failed ("the key never landed"). With the paste path's removed, the paste test failed ("the paste edited the draft but focus is None"). The other test passed in each run.
- AC #1: 30 of 30 plain runs of the pair (two loops of 15 run at the same time), on the branch rebased onto origin/dev 0923ec0ca2, at load averages of 18-60 (82-118 s per run at the peak). The whole file passed 36 of 36, the same as on dev 0923ec0ca2.

**Merge evidence (2026-10-27, branch rebased onto origin/dev 0923ec0ca2).** The review's blocking finding is fixed. The two tests passed 30 of 30 plain runs at load averages of 18-69, and the whole file passed 36 of 36 on both the branch and dev. The wait's own red/green check was run under the 0.6 s timer mutation: the old wait returned early 6 of 6 times, the new one waited 6 of 6 times. Both AC #2 mutations went red again. ./scripts/preflight.sh passes on the final head. A lesson about waits that look for a framework's stored callback is now the first entry in backlog/docs/lessons-testing-evidence.md, and the TASK-33620.15.7 entry now says its streak was luck.
