## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Tests/UI/test_console_composer_run_controls.py::test_typing_with_nothing_focused_focuses_the_draft_first and its twin test_paste_with_nothing_focused_lands_in_a_focused_draft fail on origin/dev 5b5920b8e2 in most runs (typing: 5 of 6 during TASK-33620.15.8's gates; on 2026-10-23 both failed on dev and on that branch alike) with focus back on the composer after console.set_focus(None). Cause, found with a focus probe in TASK-33620.15.8: Console activation schedules _restore_console_workbench_focus twice, after refresh and on a 0.2 s timer (ChatScreen, next to _sync_native_console_chat_ui). When the timer fires after a test's set_focus(None), the restore focuses the composer bar again (the probe saw it about 30 ms after the blur), and the tests' assert app.focused is None fails. Neither test is on the UI PR lane, so this does not block PRs, but it hides whether the keyboard and paste behaviour they pin still holds.

## Implementation Notes

**2026-10-27 (branch fix/task-33620.15.17-keys-before-first-focus).**

Re-measured first: the runs this task was filed from predate the wait that TASK-33620.15.7 added (a19334f0e6, dated 2026-10-20). It reached dev only on 2026-10-26, with the merge of PR #3211 (c398d3a4d0), and 5b5920b8e2 does not contain it. On dev a2b7104d1e, before any change, both tests passed 8 of 8 serial plain runs at load averages of 20-40.

That wait was still not the condition the tests need. It waited for the mount's 0.2 s restore timer only, and returned at once when the attach reconcile had not created the timer yet. The activation schedules three restores, all from the reconcile: after refresh and on the 0.2 s timer when the view starts, and after refresh again when the reconcile replays the screen's resume.

`_after_activation_focus_restores` (Tests/UI/test_console_composer_run_controls.py) replaces it and waits, with no fixed sleep, until:
- the attach reconcile has finished, so nothing more will be scheduled;
- one round trip through the screen's queue (`pump_probe.pump_runs`) has handed any queued after-refresh restore to the screen's callback list;
- no restore is left on that list or on a live timer;
- then one pause lets a restore's deferred `focus()` land.

- The paste path no longer does (`hand_paste_to_draft`): the paste test failed 2 of 2 ("the paste edited the draft but focus is None").
