## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Slice D3 (spec §6; ADR-086 and ADR-084 amendments accepted 2026-10-03). The adaptive reader shell gains a companion: a destination-owned widget that occupies the list region in place of the destination list. Its share of the width is `clamp(round(0.40 × available), 40, 72)`, and the reader keeps the rest. When both cannot meet their floors, the two stack as faces under a one-row header naming both. The grip names what the region holds. The slice is generic: the shell knows nothing about notes.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
