Why: The app-lookup census (`Tests/Architecture/test_app_string_lookup_census.py`, TASK-36942.4, architecture review 2026-10-25 F1) counts `getattr(app, "<name>")` / `hasattr(app, "<name>")` lookups, because a renamed or removed app service then fails at runtime instead of at import. Commit `dcc38d97c8` raised both pins to those measurements, following dev's own re-pin for lookups that predated the gate (`b07d1a1fa1`). The twelve lookups all use the `getattr(app, "<name>", None)` form the same files already used:
- `Library/library_local_rag_search_service.py`: `notes_scope_service` and `notes_user_id` (Delete forever's liveness check).
- `UI/Library_Modules/library_notes_controller.py`: `notes_scope_service` x3, `notes_service` x1, `notify` x5 and `sync_state_repository` x1 (Trash paging, the purge worker and its refusals, and the bulk Move/keyword receipts).

Until they reach their services through an explicit seam, these twelve stay outside what the census guards.
