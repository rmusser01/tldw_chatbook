## Tier-2 scope correction (2026-09-21)

**Tier 2 recommends AGAINST this task.** The per-store PRAGMA divergences are documented
per-store decisions, and one of them explicitly warns against copying it. Moving them into the shared base
would erase deliberate, recorded choices.

Also correct a claim that recurs in discussion of this cluster: "all 9 `_get_connection` overrides call
`super()._get_connection()`" is **false**. Seven do. Two -- `Notifications/event_state_repository.py:207-221`
and `DB/Library_Ingest_Jobs_DB.py` -- call `connect_private_sqlite` **directly** with
`check_same_thread=False` (documented under TASK-21131). This matters because an auditor grepping for
`super()._get_connection()` gets a **false negative** on exactly the two stores that diverge most.

Recommend closing as "won't do" with this reasoning recorded, rather than leaving it To Do.
