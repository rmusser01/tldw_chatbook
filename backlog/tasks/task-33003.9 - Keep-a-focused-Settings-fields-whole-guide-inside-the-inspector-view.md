## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
In the Phase 2 capture qa/model-config-33002-captures/settings-console-behavior-fallbacks-temperature-focused-211x44.txt, the Console Behavior Temperature fallback has focus. Its focused-field guide shows Focused setting, Purpose and Saved as above the inspector fold, but the Validation line sits below it, behind '▼ more — scroll the inspector'. The capture triage found this.

The inspector fold is not new, and neither is the focus-driven scroll that should bring the guide into view (the first-row map at UI/Screens/settings_screen.py:1861 and the scroll pass near :24535). Both exist on origin/dev 89dd84943a. With the same inspector scroll position, dev's Console Behavior guide sits in the same slot and is also below the fold. Since the TASK-33002 final review, focusing a generation fallback replaces the guide rows in place with the field-table rows. The scroll pass's own docstring names that case as the one that leaves stale scroll bounds.

A live re-drive at 211x44 could not reproduce the capture: click focus and Shift+Tab then Tab focus both showed the whole guide, with Validation at row 27 of 44. The focus route that produced the capture is still unknown. The '/' field-search jump is a candidate.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Focusing a Console Behavior generation fallback or a Providers & Models model default now keeps the field's whole Focused field guide inside the Scope Inspector's view, including after the terminal is resized.

**The route (AC#1).** Focus the field at one size, then resize the terminal. Pilot probes under the production bundle, at the capture's commit 2d16204695 and at the task base, drove click, Tab, Shift+Tab and search landing on all 14 fallbacks, and "/" search for all 14 labels (which lands on Providers & Models), at 211x44 and at 235x52. Every one left the guide fully in view. Only "focus at 235x52, then resize to 211x44" reproduced the capture, and it matched row for row. At 2d16204695 that route put "Focused setting" at y=32, "Purpose" at y=34 and "Saved as" at y=38, with "Validation" at y=40 below a 16..39 view. The capture has the same rows, and "Top P" at the top of the body because the 235x52 scroll offset (12) was kept. Focusing directly at 211x44 put the guide at y=18..27. The Phase 2 captures were taken at both sizes, and this one came after a resize. At base (ddbcf282ba) the route is worse. Task 7's one-row rhythm lets the whole body fit at 235x52, so the offset carried into 211x44 is 0, and after the resize to 211x44, only "Focused setting" and the first line of "Purpose" are still in view. Providers & Models fails the other way. Focus at 211x44, resize to 235x52: the rows above the guide un-wrap, and "Focused setting" moves above the top edge (y=14 against a view starting at 15).
Reproduction: `Tests/UI/test_settings_field_guide_in_view.py` (the `_resize_both_ways` step). Live: `qa/model-config-p3-2026-09-28/task-9/live_drive.sh` against a base tree; `base-cb-temperature-resized-211x44.txt` shows the guide cut after the first line of "Purpose", and `base-pm-temperature-resized-235x52.txt` has no "Focused setting" row.

**Root cause.** `_scroll_impact_pane_to_field_guide` pins the guide only when focus changes. A resize re-wraps the inspector's prose but keeps its scroll offset, and nothing ran the pin again.

**Fix** (`UI/Screens/settings_screen.py`, +5 lines). `_reveal_settings_focus_after_refresh` is the hook both layout-change paths already call to keep the focused control visible: `on_resize`, and the Providers & Models draft-status update while a model default is typed into. It now also re-pins the guide when the focused widget is the field the inspector names (`_active_settings_field_id`). The fix sits in the shared hook, so Appearance, Storage and Library/RAG get the same behaviour. The pin itself is unchanged.
- Ruling: re-pin inside `_reveal_settings_focus_after_refresh`, not only in `on_resize`. Why: it is the one hook for "layout changed, focus kept", and its other caller (typing on Providers & Models) is the header-grows case its own comment names. Cost if wrong: while typing in a model default, a manual inspector scroll snaps back to the guide on the next keystroke.
- Ruling: for Console Behavior, the "/" route is covered by `_land_search_focus_on_field`, the landing that "/" + Enter runs, and not by typing a query. Why: every generation label is also indexed on Providers & Models, which ranks first (same tier, earlier in the rail), so "/" + a label always opens Providers & Models. That route is tested for real in the second test. Cost if wrong: none for the fix; only the typing part of the route is untested for Console Behavior.
- Ruling: Providers & Models coverage is the 10 model defaults the test provider shows (OpenAI gpt-5.6-terra). Min P, Top K, Thinking and Thinking budget are hidden and disabled for that model (TASK-33001.2), so no focus route can reach them. They share the same guide rows and the same pin. Cost if wrong: a second provider run, about 45 s more test time.

**Tests (AC#4)** — new `Tests/UI/test_settings_field_guide_in_view.py`, 10 nodes, production bundle (`StyledSettingsDestinationHarness`), 211x44 and 235x52. Every test is wrapped in `@private_profile_test`, so a plain local run (and CI) runs each node in a child pytest on a fresh profile instead of failing setup with ADR-126 `raw_source_selection_changed`:
- `test_console_behavior_fallback_guide_stays_in_the_inspector_view`: clicks all 14 fallbacks (a clicked Select's list is closed with Esc), Tabs through all 14, lands on each by search landing, then resizes to the other size and back.
- `test_model_default_guide_stays_in_the_inspector_view`: "/" + label + Enter for every model default (Esc first, so "/" is not typed into the previous Input), clicks and Tabs through every one shown, then resizes both ways.
- `test_other_guided_categories_keep_the_guide_in_view_on_resize` (fix round 1): Appearance (Palette limit), Storage (ChaChaNotes DB) and Library/RAG (the profile picker; a fresh profile's built-in RAG profile is read-only, so its retrieval fields are disabled). It focuses the field, then resizes both ways. Every guide row must stay in view, and the first row must head the view again, as focus left it, unless the pane cannot scroll that far. These guides stay in view at base too; the re-pin check is what shows the shared hook reaches them.
- Each Console Behavior and Providers & Models check reads every guide row and requires "Focused setting: <the focused field's label>" first, "Validation: …" last, and every row inside the body's `scrollable_content_region`.
- Red at base (whose only production difference is this fix): 7 of 10. Both Console Behavior sizes and Providers & Models at 211x44 fail on the resize step, while click, Tab and search pass. Appearance passes at base, because nothing above its guide re-wraps, so the kept offset still pins it. Green at head: 10 of 10.
