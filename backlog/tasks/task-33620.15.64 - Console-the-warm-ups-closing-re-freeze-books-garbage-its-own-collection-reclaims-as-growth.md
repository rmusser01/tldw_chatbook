## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found in TASK-33620.15.57's review (2026-11-06). Before the refreezer's first look, IdleHeapRefreezer.refreeze_now counts the unfrozen generations before its full collection runs, so cyclic garbage present at the warm-up's end is booked as frozen-set growth although the collection then reclaims it. Live it was harmless: 29.2-29.4K objects booked against budgets of 160-185K in all 36 launches of round b1. But a garbage-heavy moment at the warm-up's end could make a compaction due that the frozen set does not justify, and a compaction holds the UI loop for 241-298 ms (TASK-33620.15.57's description). refreeze_long_lived_heap already counts the old generation after its collection, but only for its DEBUG line, and returns nothing to the refreezer.
