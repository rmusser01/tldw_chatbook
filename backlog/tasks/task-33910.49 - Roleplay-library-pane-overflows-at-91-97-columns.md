## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
- **Present on dev 29592ecc9b, worse than the title; this PR fixes it.** The compact cut-off was 90, but the 2:4:2 split's CSS minimums (library 24, work area 40, inspector 30, `css/components/_agentic_terminal.tcss`) plus the workbench's 4 border and padding cells need 98 columns. From 91 to 97 Textual could not honour the minimums, and the library painted 174-186 columns wide (work area 348-372, inspector 174-186), so Import…, + New, the work area and the Inspector were all off-screen. The styled mock tier and a real `TldwCli` measured the same.
- **Fix.** `PERSONAS_COMPACT_WORKBENCH_MAX_WIDTH = 24 + 40 + 30 + 4 - 1` (97), with a comment naming the CSS it follows. At 91-97 Roleplay now shows the compact split dev shows at 90. `personas_screen.py` stays at 16,829 lines, so its ratchet row is untouched.
- **Drift guard (AC#3).** `test_compact_cut_off_is_the_widest_width_the_minimums_cannot_fill` mounts Roleplay at 120 columns and reads each pane's resolved minimum width and margins and the workbench's chrome. It requires the cut-off to equal their sum minus one, so changing a minimum, a margin, or the workbench's border or padding fails until the constant follows (mutation M49-D).
- **Tests** (`Tests/UI/test_personas_workbench.py::TestWorkbenchShell`):
  - `test_workbench_panes_and_library_controls_fit_at_width` covers 80, 89, 90, 91, 94, 97, 98, 99, 120, 160 and 220 under both styled tiers, 22 cases. It checks the panes fit, the library's Import…, search, Name, Tag, + New and rows paint whole inside the pane, and the columns match a table. The table's rows at or below 90 and from 98 up were measured on dev and pass there; its 91/94/97 rows were measured on this branch.
  - `test_workbench_panes_fit_at_every_width_from_80_to_220` resizes one app through every width from 80 to 220 and checks fit and controls at each.
- **Known limitation, not fixed here.** The compact split middle-elides list names and clips some Inspector button labels. This exists on dev at 80-90 and is now also what 91-97 show. B7 (TASK-33910.12) re-tiers this layout and replaces the constant.
- **Files:** `tldw_chatbook/UI/Screens/personas_screen.py`, `Tests/UI/test_personas_workbench.py`, `Docs/User_Guide/roleplay-chat-dictionaries.md` (one paragraph on the narrow split).
