## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found in review of TASK-33620.15.5 and TASK-33620.15.14. The post-UI-ready pre-import pass imports the Console's first-send modules after the last screen route, on its daemon thread. On established profiles that window starts a median 2.15 s after the Console's first frame (1.74-6.15 s over 72 fix-arm launches; 8.1-9.0 s on a fresh-profile smoke) and lasts about 0.3 s. TASK-33620.15.14 sampled navigation at 1, 3 and 6 s and single keys at 0.5-6 s, and none of its 656 fix-arm inputs landed inside that window, so a click or key that overlaps one of the warm-up's imports (they share the GIL with the UI loop; Canvas.native_authority is the longest) is unmeasured. Route imports, the same kind of daemon work, cost keys 3-7 ms, so the risk is low but unproven. A first send made before the pass finishes also gets none of the warm-up's gain (it pays the imports on the UI loop, as on origin/dev); that is not a regression, but nothing measured how often a first send arrives that early.
