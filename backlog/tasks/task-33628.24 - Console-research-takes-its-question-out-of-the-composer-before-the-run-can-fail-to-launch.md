## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found in the PR #3458 review of TASK-33628.20 (2026-11-12). `/research <question>` takes its text once it has queued its run worker and posted "Deep research started" (`ChatScreen._console_command_research`). The run itself launches inside that worker (`local_service.launch_run`, then `engine.execute_run`). A launch failure there, such as the corrupt or unreadable research store from TASK-21105, is only logged as a warning. So the question is gone from the composer and the chat says the research started, but no run exists and nothing says so. Before TASK-33628.20 the text stayed in the composer, so the question could at least be sent again.
