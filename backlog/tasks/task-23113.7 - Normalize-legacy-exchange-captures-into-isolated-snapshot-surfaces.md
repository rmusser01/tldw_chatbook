## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Expose historical full and safe exchange blobs through isolated legacy snapshot surfaces with explicit uncertainty and without inventing semantic chronology.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Existing full and safe capture blobs normalize incrementally into isolated `legacy_snapshot` surfaces structurally equivalent to their sanitized legacy projection or an explicit omission record.
- [ ] Repeated transcript prefixes in legacy snapshots use persistent prefix nodes so migration does not recreate quadratic normalized storage.
- [ ] Unchanged pre-ledger messages acquire lazy digest-free revisions and normalize as canonical references when uniquely matched instead of becoming provider-only artifacts.
- [ ] The viewer labels legacy provenance and uncertainty and never presents inferred message edits, call order, or source identity as known fact.
- [ ] Migration is resumable and idempotent with batches bounded to 100 rows, 100 ms, and 4 MiB and completes the five-second historical fixture gate.
- [ ] Unmigrated and migrated records remain readable during rollout, including malformed or partially recognized legacy blobs.
- [ ] ADR required: yes; this task implements the legacy compatibility policy in ADR-097.
