## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
On dev today, Enter in the Roleplay search box within the 0.2 s debounce puts the cursor on the previous result's first row, so the four-key find (Ctrl+F, a name, Enter, Enter) can open the wrong card. Roleplay frame B2a (TASK-33910.3) fixes that by having Enter wait for its search's render. The wait runs inside the screen's message handler, and that holds every non-priority key and message on Roleplay while it waits. Locally that is the render (about 30-40 ms); on a server source it is the server's round trip. The hold is new with B2a. B2a's final review bounded it at 2 s (`_SEARCH_SUBMIT_WAIT_SECONDS`): after that Enter does what dev did (the cursor goes to the first row shown) and the slow search still lands its rows. B2a does not remove the hold: on a server slower than 2 s, the four-key find's second Enter may act on the previous results, as on dev.

The design to build: Enter records "land on the first match" and returns at once; the list applies it when the search's rows arrive; Enter on the list stays inert until they do.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 No key or message on Roleplay waits on a search render: a key pressed right after Enter in the search box is handled at once, whatever the server's latency

- [ ] #3 Enter on the list while the search's rows are pending opens nothing, and the cursor lands on the first match when they arrive
- [ ] #4 The 2 s bound and its fallback to dev's behaviour are gone
