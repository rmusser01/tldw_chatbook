## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found while measuring TASK-33620.15.1.4 live on origin/dev 3be493413a (isolated profiles, Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 through the review proxy, 235x52, Inspect rail open, host load 8-16). In the 3 s after each of 12 run completions, ChatScreen._sync_console_rail_and_controls (the 0.2 s poll tick's projection) took 37-159 ms of UI-loop time (34-78 ms CPU), up to 107 ms in a single call; its callback reached 100-237 ms when a generation-2 pass landed in it. ChatScreen._current_console_rail_state, a full rail-state build (inspector state, workspace context state, provider selection) behind one projection, was a median 29 ms of each completion and spiked to 165-437 ms wall on 47-64 ms CPU at load 16, so most of a spike is waiting (storage admission and database reads on the loop). The same build runs inside _set_console_rail_preference on every rail open and close (7-10 ms median, 437 ms once at load 16), and ConsoleAgentController._console_agent_section_payload builds it again for one field, agent_open, in each coalesced Agent-fleet sync. Over the whole session the projection ran 210 times with a median of 7.8 ms, p90 49.6 ms and max 589 ms. At load 30-40 the completion projection took 127-208 ms per completion. In a paired round at load 15-30 the callbacks of 100 ms or more at a completion included 9 poll ticks (6 of them in this projection, 105-158 ms), 4 coalesced Agent-fleet syncs (107-143 ms, which also pay TASK-33620.15.1.6's consent reads) and 2 native Console syncs run as async workers (111-209 ms). Evidence: p14pt-T*.jsonl with p14tm.py and p14cbtm.py in tools/evidence/bd5/P14 (the live probe kit beside pf2probe/).
