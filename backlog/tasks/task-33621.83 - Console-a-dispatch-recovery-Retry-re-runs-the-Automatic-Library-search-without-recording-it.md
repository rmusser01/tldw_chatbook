## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
TASK-33621.51 records what each Automatic Library search did on its USER turn and shows it as one line under the turn. The dispatch-recovery Retry (settlement or handoff failure, unknown-delivery recovery) builds its request in _resolve_dispatch_retry_context: whenever the policy is Automatic it runs the search again, may send fresh evidence, and writes no record. The turn keeps its first record, so after a Retry the line can read 'sent once without Library · search timed out' or '0 matches' over a reply that was grounded. The retry also ignores an earlier 'Send once without Library' choice for that turn. Found in the review of TASK-33621.51; the path is rare but the line then states something false.
