## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES:BEGIN -->
Deferred the annotation-only coordinator import in Plugins/admission.py and the inspector import in Plugins/review.py to their existing type/use sites. Ordinary PluginService construction avoids package-management modules; service identity, real worker/inspection, admission and run-history protections remain unchanged. Existing [ADR-097](../decisions/097-boot-budget-ratchets.md) and [ADR-162](../decisions/162-managed-agent-plugins.md) apply; no new boundary or ADR.

The fresh-interpreter regression failed on the original imports, then 20 targeted plugin/regression controls passed. The unchanged regression now lives in Tests/Performance/test_app_import_weight.py, already selected by Perf Guard. Its final rerun and the original import-budget guard passed (679/686 modules, unchanged ceiling). Two inherited lint findings were fixed with byte-identical generated import code. Ruff lint/format and whitespace checks pass; independent code and measurement reviews found no blocker.

Balanced, profiler-free comparison: four fresh apps per arm, three DeepSeek Flash sends each. Measured baseline 14e13 vs candidate acb96 had first-send medians of 4.443 to 4.022 s input-to-request and 2.295 to 2.169 s post-save preparation; later medians were 2.892 to 2.589 s and 1.144 to 1.039 s. Only two of four first-send pairs improved under host load around 14-16 on 18 CPUs: the observed 126 ms/5.49% post-save median reduction is not a reliable causal/general speedup estimate. All 24 requests, exact prompts/replies and linked traces passed; settings and all 27 tool schemas matched, zero checkpoints remained, and all eight profiles passed process/port cleanup audits.

Raw packets merged-send-latency-r1 and paired-plugin-import-latency-r1 and independent reports are retained in the local task evidence directory. Final test-owner/documentation edits preserve every production Python byte measured at acb96. TASK-34406 remains In Progress; its broad latency targets are unqualified. No full-suite, population p95, native-paint or Windows acceptance is claimed.
