## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Define exclusive starred-first conversation ownership, independent Workspaces and Conversations search state, and bounded per-workspace paging with stale-result and Retry protection.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 A pure deterministic projection assigns every named-workspace conversation to exactly one workspace node and every Default/unassigned conversation to exactly one flat row; moves between owners apply atomically with no missing or duplicate record.
- [ ] #2 Starred records remain in their one owner, sort before unstarred records with recency as the secondary key, and never create a Starred aggregate or duplicate ordinary/search result.
- [ ] #3 Workspaces and Conversations own separate query, debounce, attempt-generation, result/error, cache, Retry, and worker state; actions or completions in one projection cannot cancel or overwrite the other.
- [ ] #4 Expanded workspaces load bounded service pages with independent per-workspace attempt generations and cursors; same-cursor overlap commits once, stale membership/query/mount results are discarded, and search finds service-scope matches not yet materialized.
- [ ] #5 Search and page failures preserve the last settled projection or loaded children, expose the correct scoped Retry target, replace failed generations on Retry, and remain independent of one another.
- [ ] #6 Before projection production edits, deterministic small, representative, and stress fixtures capture an immutable checksummed raw baseline with total service and materialized-row counts; the same fixtures prove deterministic ordering, page boundaries, selected/active/run markers, literal titles, and incremental non-structural updates without querying or rebuilding unrelated owners.
<!-- AC:END -->
