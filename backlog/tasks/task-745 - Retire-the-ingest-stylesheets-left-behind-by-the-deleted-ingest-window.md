## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Two ingest stylesheets remain in the CSS bundle after the standalone ingest window was deleted: they style that window's forms and tab strip, and sampling their selectors finds almost none still referenced from Python. They are harmless where they are -- unused selectors cost nothing at runtime -- but they are dead weight that misleads anyone reading the bundle for what the ingest UI looks like. Removing them is deliberately separate from the window deletion because they are part of the generated bundle, so a removal needs the bundle regenerated and the remaining ingest surface eyeballed for any selector that turns out to be shared.
