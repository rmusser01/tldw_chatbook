The polish-1b finishing pass then found a live defect of the same class. Held-import walk at 120x36: pressing `x` on a Trash row, then the import ending, moved focus from Old draft 25 to Old draft 21 in 2 of 10 runs (load 40-50). The `x` case was fixed in `5dbef1bc39`. The wider class stays: any Trash row can lose focus to a grip capture between a recompose and its replay, with or without `x`. Parking focus on the grip before the release makes it land on the first row deterministically.
