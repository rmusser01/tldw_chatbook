## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
**Setup.** The file now carries `pytestmark = pytest.mark.bootstrap_profile`, as its sibling mounted Console files do (the per-test sandbox refuses ChatScreen's config admission with `RecoveryRequired`). With it, at base dev b31e3e9241, 7 whole-file `-n 4` runs at load 37-80 failed 3 to 7 nodes each: staged owner sync and TASK-24705's responsive hide/reveal in all 7, then `empty_owner` 6, `navigation_focuses_overflow_viewport` 3, `structural_control` 3, `disappearing_target` 2, `inactive_section_history` 2, `newer_navigation` 1, `local_offset` 1. The file runs on no PR lane, so no CI log exists for any of them.

**1. `test_staged_owner_sync_drives_ten_eleven_ten_cue_and_clamp`: test drift after a named harness change.** The fixture sized the right rail at 19 rows so ten sources fill the outer body exactly. A probe measured 17 content rows: the rail has `.console-region`'s one-row padding top and bottom, and the Sources tray has `.console-inspector-context-section`'s one-row bottom margin, so the ten-source tray (11 rows plus margin) overflows and the wait for "ten-source counterfactual fit" times out. A first-parent probe of dev from 3105d2b5ea to b31e3e9241 found the padding first at 8ca6b1474065f8ae51284890af01b1b006d74f76 (PR #2050, 2026-08-28; its parent 0b882a28f08e3074d207b528c1132e1053f36a3c measures padding 0 and 19 content rows). That merge brought 09dfb3b9bc883e6ed2ebdb59b1b3d689f50c1f9c, which set `ConsoleHarness.CSS_PATH = str(BUNDLED_STYLESHEET)`; at 8ca6b14740 with that one line removed the probe measures padding 0 and 19 rows again. The test was written against a harness that loaded no bundled stylesheet; the product's rail has always had these spacings in the app. (An earlier first-parent bisect of 61a71e5305..a0b8f96416 found the test first red at d7bb844d9bf567eaa3f2b6891e3b9a560f4409f5, PR #2143, at the eleven-source stage; that stage passes today.) Fix: the fixture CSS zeroes the rail's padding and the tray's margin; every wait and assertion, including the `(1, 1, 6, 11, 0)` / `(1, 1, 6, 10, 1)` row tuples, is unchanged.

**2. Focus-history race (test race): `test_empty_owner_is_settled_absence_with_one_outer_fallback`, `test_structural_control_and_boundary_removal_recovers_without_stale_reentry`, and the same shape in `disappearing_target`, `inactive_section_history`, `persistent_malformed_owner`, `structural_run_recompose`, `recovery_uses_external_header_control` and `recovery_falls_back_to_outer_body_then_collapse`.** Each focused a control, waited for `pilot.app.focused is control`, then removed or recomposed it and waited for the rail's recovery. `Screen.set_focus` sets `app.focused` at once; the rail records the section's focus history in `on_descendant_focus`, a message delivered later. Removed first, the control leaves no history to recover from. Load evidence: an instrumented copy of `empty_owner` failed 5 of 12 at load ~55; each failing rep had no recovery incident after the removal and focus stayed on Textual's fallback (Environment's "view all"), while passing reps logged the incident and reached the outer body about 0.77 s later. Forced delay: a throwaway plugin wrapping `ConsoleInspectorRail.on_descendant_focus` in `await asyncio.sleep(0.3)` failed 8 of the 9 base cases at the same lines as the natural failures (external header passed); `test_focus_leave_clears_recovery_incident_before_mutation_and_reentry`, which already waited on `rail._section_focus_history`, passed. Fix: `_focus_captured_by_rail` focuses and waits until `app.focused` is the control and the rail's history for its section names it; all 9 cases pass under the same delay, except `structural_control`'s second stage (see below).

**3. `test_n_and_p_are_rail_local_no_wrap_and_editable_input_keeps_printable_key` (line 486): test race.** It asserted the scroll-owner underline right after `app.focused` changed, but the rail paints that cue from the same `on_descendant_focus`. Not seen in 7 base runs; under the forced delay it fails at `assert "underline" in str(collapse.styles.text_style)` (`'underline' in 'none'`). Fix: both waits (outer body, then Sources after `n`) also wait for the cue; the asserts stay.

**4. `test_navigation_focuses_overflow_viewport_contains_header_and_preserves_state` (line 640): product defect, fixed in `UI/Console_Modules/right_rail.py`.** `_focus_boundary` called `target.focus()`, scrolled the header to the top, and queued a two-pass reveal that stops once focus is not on its target. Textual applies the focus later and then queues `scroll_to_center(target, origin_visible=True)`, which for an overflowing viewport scrolls the outer body so the viewport's top shows, pushing the header out by a row. A probe at load ~40 failed 2 of 12: in one the reveal's first check ran while focus was still on the outer body (chain stopped) and the scroll went 5 to 6 when focus landed; in the other both reveal passes ran and the scroll went 5 to 6 after them. Fix: `target.focus(scroll_visible=False)`, so the reveal is the only scroll (as `_focus_library_activity` already does, and as lessons-textual's TASK-40318 entry on `focus()`'s scroll advises). New test `test_navigation_header_stays_revealed_when_focus_lands_after_the_reveal` forces the losing order by running the reveal at once: red 4 of 4 without the fix (`Sources header at y=14 is outside the outer body Region(x=128, y=15, width=31, height=9) (scroll_y=6)`), green 3 of 3 with it. `test_newer_navigation_prevents_stale_delayed_header_reveal`'s one failure (`62.875... == 63.875...`, the outer offset moving one row after it settled) is the same late focus scroll; it is not edited.

**5. `test_local_offset_survives_collapse_reopen_without_extra_writes`: test race.** It clicked the open control straight after the collapse click, with neither click's result checked; under load the second click landed before the open control appeared and the rail stayed closed. Fix: assert the collapse click, then `click_inspect_rail_open` (TASK-42416's helper in `Tests/UI/test_console_shell_regions.py`, imported, not edited), which waits until the control is clickable and the rail is open. `_open_inspector` uses the same helper for every test's first open.

**Not fixed here.** `test_responsive_hide_and_reveal_hands_off_focus_without_losing_offset` stays red, owned by TASK-24705, and is left out of the lane. Under the 0.3 s forced delay `structural_control`'s second stage (removing the whole boundary right after the recovery focused `current_inserted`) can still lose its recovery: the rail's late `on_descendant_focus` for a control that has since been removed clears the history the recovery wrote. Not seen without the forced delay (0 of 20 soak runs). Proposed as a follow-up.
