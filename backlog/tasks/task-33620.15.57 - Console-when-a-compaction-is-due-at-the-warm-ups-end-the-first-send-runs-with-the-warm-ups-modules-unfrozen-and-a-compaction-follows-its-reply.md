## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found in TASK-33620.15.51's live rounds (2026-11-05, origin/dev 29592ecc9b, real Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5, one first send per launch 4.5 s after the first-send warm-up ended). Since PR #3336 (ADR-198's 2026-11-01 amendment), the warm-up's end requests the Console's idle maintenance instead of freezing the heap itself. The request's timer fires 2 s later and re-freezes. In 4 of 36 dev launches, the timer found a compaction already due instead. Its growth budget is the larger of 50,000 objects and 25% of the frozen baseline, and runtime re-freezes count what they freeze as growth. The timer then waited for 5 s of quiet, and the first send dropped the request. The send ran with the warm-up's modules still unfrozen: main-thread GC in Enter to POST was a median 69 ms (45-80) in the 11 such launches across the arms that have the change, against 45 ms in the 53 where the re-freeze ran (host load not matched). The first reply's maintenance then compacted on the UI loop (unfreeze, a full collection of the whole heap, freeze): 241-298 ms on dev, 11.8-13.5 s after the warm-up ended. Launches that skipped the re-freeze, by arm: dev 4 of 36 (all 4 compacted); dev with the walking boot freeze restored 7 of 20 (5 compacted); dev with the old warm-up steps restored 3 of 19 (3); dev with the old closing boot freeze restored 0 of 19; dev with all of #3336's runtime changes reverted 0 of 17. So the cause is the completion change, and the timing of the boot freezes before it changes how often it happens. Before #3336, the closing boot freeze froze the warm-up's modules on the pre-import thread at the pass's end, and counted them into the baseline rather than as growth. A compaction runs only after 5 s with no input, but a key or click that lands during one waits for it. Overall, dev's first send was not later than without #3336 (TASK-33620.15.51), so this is a minority-of-launches cost. TASK-33620.15.50 is why the warm-up's modules stopped being boot freezes.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
The first-send warm-up's completion now re-freezes the heap on the UI loop at once, and the Console's idle maintenance waits while a pre-import pass runs. So a first send finds the warm-up's modules frozen, and neither the routes nor the warm-up can book growth that makes a compaction due. ADR-198 has a 2026-11-06 amendment.

**Attribution (live, dev 2684e5453f, round b1).** The p51 probe was extended (G57 kit) to record the refreezer's accounting at every budget check. The Console's paint request re-freezes 2 s after the Console first paints a conversation. When that lands inside the screen-route pass, it freezes the routes imported so far as growth, and the pass's boot freeze credits only the rest. The warm-up's request then books the warm-up's own ~29K as growth on top. A runtime maintenance ran inside the pass in 16 of 36 dev launches (18 of 36 with #3336's runtime changes reverted). The closer to the pass's end it ran, the more it booked: 110K at 0.86 s before the end, against a 144K budget. Two revert launches at 0.15-0.16 s came within 21-26K of their budgets, less than the 29K that dev adds. On 29592ecc9b (TASK-33620.15.51's rounds, re-read here), all 4 compacting dev launches had run that re-freeze 0.03-0.25 s before the pass ended. In b1, no dev launch ran in that band.

**Fix** (`Utils/ui_responsiveness.py`, `UI/Console_Modules/transcript.py`, `Chat/console_first_send_modules.py`, `app.py`, `app_idle_warmup.py`):
- `_console_heap_busy` also holds while `_screen_preimport_thread` or `_initial_screen_preimport_thread` is alive, so a pass's modules are frozen only by its boot freeze. `_console_foreground_busy` is the old check.
- The completion is a step: `_warm_first_send` parks and waits out input (`_pause(app, 0.0)`) before `call_from_thread`. On an idle attached Console, `finish_console_heap_warm_up` calls `IdleHeapRefreezer.refreeze_now`. That is a runtime re-freeze: a full collection, then a freeze, with what it froze counted as growth (TASK-33620.15.50 stands). It does not walk the frozen set: before the first look it counts the unfrozen generations into `_unlooked_growth`, and after it marks the size stale. It runs even with a compaction owed, which stays owed, and it satisfies a pending request.
- It requests idle maintenance only when it could not re-freeze (held, busy, off) or a stranded compaction is owed. Deviation from plan step 3b: the first version also requested maintenance as before. Live, that second re-freeze collected a near-empty heap, and the first send then ran two UI-loop generation-2 passes where dev ran one or none. That incident is in lessons-live-verification.md.
- Unchanged per the owner's ruling: the TASK-33620.15.3 compaction floor (50K) and budget, the quiet times, and the stranded policy.

**AC #1 (round b1, `tools/evidence/bd5/G57/b1`, `rows-clean.json`).** Real Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 through the review proxy, 160x45, Enter 4.5 s after the warm-up's end, and a key injected the moment the first post-POST maintenance started. Launches were gated on the load generators (`signal.alarm`) only, and b25 was re-run after one started inside it. An idle-CPU gate of 55%, then 30%, never opened in 60 min of other sessions' pytest. Idle CPU at the send was 0-95%: 20 blocks were at 15% or more in every arm, and 16 at 0-14% (mostly 0).
- Warm-up modules unfrozen at Enter: dev 0/36, revert 0/36, fix 0/36.
- First reply's idle maintenance compacted: 0/36 in every arm, and no compaction ran after the POST at all. So there was no compaction to time, and no key landed in one. The first post-POST maintenance was a re-freeze in all 108 launches (medians 20.9-22.0 ms), and the key sent into it paid 35.0 (dev), 36.0 (revert) and 36.1 ms (fix), injection to frame.

**AC #2 (fix, same round).**
- The fix ran no runtime maintenance inside a pass (0/36).
- The first budget check after the warm-up booked 29,166-29,425 objects of growth in all 36 launches, below the 50K floor, against budgets of 160-185K. Foreground objects frozen by the closing re-freeze still count as growth: `test_live_foreground_objects_a_refreeze_now_freezes_count_as_growth` checks this with the real collector.
- The closing re-freeze took 3.1-11.3 ms on the UI loop (median 4.1).
- Enter to POST, paired: fix-dev -72 ms (bootstrap 95% CI -130 to +6), fix-revert +10 (-130 to +38). Main-thread GC in that span: -1.8 ms (-9.1 to +2.4) and -3.5 (-11.4 to +4.4).
- Send-window generation-2 passes: 2 or more in 12 (fix), 14 (dev) and 15 (revert) launches. With the host at least 15% idle, the median total was 6.7 ms (fix), 15.4 (dev) and 13.9 (revert).

A likely cause, not measured: what the 2 s after the warm-up allocate stays unfrozen until the first reply. The fix's Enter to POST in those 16 loaded blocks was still a paired median 108 ms sooner than dev's. Filed as TASK-33620.15.60.
