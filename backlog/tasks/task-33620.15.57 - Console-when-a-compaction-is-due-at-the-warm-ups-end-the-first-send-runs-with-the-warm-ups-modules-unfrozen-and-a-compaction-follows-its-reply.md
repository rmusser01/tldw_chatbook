## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found in TASK-33620.15.51's live rounds (2026-11-05, origin/dev 29592ecc9b, real Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5, one first send per launch 4.5 s after the first-send warm-up ended). Since PR #3336 (ADR-198's 2026-11-01 amendment), the warm-up's end requests the Console's idle maintenance instead of freezing the heap itself. The request's timer fires 2 s later and re-freezes. In 4 of 36 dev launches, the timer found a compaction already due instead. Its growth budget is the larger of 50,000 objects and 25% of the frozen baseline, and runtime re-freezes count what they freeze as growth. The timer then waited for 5 s of quiet, and the first send dropped the request. The send ran with the warm-up's modules still unfrozen: main-thread GC in Enter to POST was a median 69 ms (45-80) in the 11 such launches across the arms that have the change, against 45 ms in the 53 where the re-freeze ran (host load not matched). The first reply's maintenance then compacted on the UI loop (unfreeze, a full collection of the whole heap, freeze): 241-298 ms on dev, 11.8-13.5 s after the warm-up ended. Launches that skipped the re-freeze, by arm: dev 4 of 36 (all 4 compacted); dev with the walking boot freeze restored 7 of 20 (5 compacted); dev with the old warm-up steps restored 3 of 19 (3); dev with the old closing boot freeze restored 0 of 19; dev with all of #3336's runtime changes reverted 0 of 17. So the cause is the completion change, and the timing of the boot freezes before it changes how often it happens. Before #3336, the closing boot freeze froze the warm-up's modules on the pre-import thread at the pass's end, and counted them into the baseline rather than as growth. A compaction runs only after 5 s with no input, but a key or click that lands during one waits for it. Overall, dev's first send was not later than without #3336 (TASK-33620.15.51), so this is a minority-of-launches cost. TASK-33620.15.50 is why the warm-up's modules stopped being boot freezes.
