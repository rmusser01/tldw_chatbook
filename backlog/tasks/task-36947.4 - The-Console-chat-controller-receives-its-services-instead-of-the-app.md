## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
`ConsoleChatController` exposes a settable `self.app` (assigned by the chat screen and the launch-wake path). It reads at least 18 app attributes and forwards the whole app into five helpers.

Source: architecture review 2026-10-25, `Docs/QA/architecture-review-2026-10-25/report.md` §F1; claim-by-claim verification in `Docs/QA/architecture-review-2026-10-25/verification.md` (origin/dev 7a5e2513c5).

**Re-scoped 2026-11-15.** The owner decided on 2026-11-12 to "park and re-plan".

**Where the work stands.** PR #3476 (branch `arch/p3-console-controller`, GitHub head f33962e576, based on dev e78b62f0e7) met the original criteria against that base:
- The controller takes a frozen `ConsoleControllerHost` instead of the app.
- Six helpers it reached no longer hold the app.
- A reachability guard walks what the controller reaches.
- About 160 test files build the controller through one shared factory.
- `HeadlessAppContext` shrank to the two attributes the mobile resume helpers still read.

The PR stays open, unmerged, as the starting point. Its body and review rounds record the design.

**Why it no longer fits.** After #3476 was written, #3480 (TASK-39147, merged 2026-11-12) anchored per-turn source custody on the app, and reaches that app through the controller. The custody checks refuse a turn whose services were swapped mid-turn. They take the app from `controller.app` and keep it for the whole turn:
- `Agents/ordinary_tree.py`: `OrdinaryTurn` holds `controller.app`, and the controller keeps the tree in `_ordinary_submissions` for the turn.
- 20 `tree.app` identity and static-attribute proofs in `Chat/console_ordinary_tree.py`.
- `Agents/native_source_storage.py`.
- The MCP capture witness in `Chat/console_configuration_preparation.py`.
- `Chat/console_workflow_preparation.py` and `Chat/console_reference_preparation.py`.
- Eight `controller.app` reads in `Chat/console_workflow_runs.py`, six of them `controller.app.console_runtime is not <runtime>` identity checks.
- Tests that pin a mid-turn swap through the controller's app: `Tests/Plugins/test_owned_hook_acceptance.py` (`changed_mcp_source`) and `Tests/Chat/test_console_ordinary_tree.py`.

**Size, re-measured on dev ecad404bd4 (2026-11-15).** #3476's two `controller.app` scans (pure AST, run verbatim from f33962e576 over git trees: `test_no_module_reads_the_console_controllers_app` over tldw_chatbook/ and `test_no_test_sets_or_reads_the_console_controllers_app` over Tests/) find 505 sites, against 433 at #3476's base e78b62f0e7 (net +72):
- **40 new production sites in 14 modules:**
  - `console_provider_composition` 12
  - `console_workflow_runs` 8
  - `console_workflow_preparation` 4
  - `console_plugin_hook_preparation` 3
  - 2 each: `console_configuration_preparation`, `Agents/native_source_storage` and `Agents/workflow_phase`
  - 1 each: `console_reference_preparation`, `console_chat_start`, `console_tool_surface_preview`, `Agents/workflow_task_scope`, `Agents/workflow_run_service`, `Agents/ordinary_tree` and `UI/Console_Modules/wiring.py`
- **35 new test sites in 20 files**, and 3 fewer in `Tests/UI/test_console_runtime_ownership.py`.

The scans do not count the `tree.app` proofs, or the custody modules that take the app from `runtime._app`.

**The conflict.** #3476's invariant (nothing the controller reaches holds the app) and #3480's checks cannot both hold as written. There are three known ways to reconcile them:
- **(a) Take the app from `runtime._app`.** #3480's checks stay byte for byte, but the per-turn objects then hold the app while the controller reaches them.
- **(b) Anchor the checks on the controller's host.** This changes #3480's behaviour: host members are frozen, `getattr_static` proofs become plain reads, and the swap-refusal tests are rewritten.
- **(c) Move the app-anchored proofs behind runtime-built callables.** This follows #3476's capture-callable precedent, and is a redesign of #3480's custody modules.

Choosing one is the implementation plan's call, made with the owner when this task starts.

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 The controller holds no reference to the app object
- [ ] #2 Every service it used through the app arrives through its construction
- [ ] #3 No helper it calls receives the app object
- [ ] #4 No module or test under tldw_chatbook/ or Tests/ sets or reads a controller's `app`, per #3476's AST scan run on the merge head
- [ ] #5 A turn whose services are swapped mid-turn is still refused, or the task notes record the owner's ruling that changes that behaviour
- [ ] #6 Before code changes, the implementation plan records which reconciliation was chosen ((a), (b), (c) or another) and the owner's agreement
- [ ] #7 Any object the controller reaches during a turn that still holds the app is named in the task notes, with the owner's ruling that allows it as an exception to #1 and #3
