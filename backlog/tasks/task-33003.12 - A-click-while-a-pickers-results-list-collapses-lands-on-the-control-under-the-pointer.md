## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Closed without product code: AC#1 holds at HEAD for real input, and the rider's "lands on whatever moved under the pointer" is `Pilot.click`'s own behaviour, not the app's. A new pilot test pins AC#1 without Escape.

Reproduced first, as the plan asked, on Settings ▸ Providers & Models at 211x44 (a temporary probe under Tests/UI, deleted): with the Default model list open (12 catalog ids, 10 rows), a `pilot.click` on Generation defaults' title, or on the Temperature field, focused the pressed control, the blur timer (0.05 s) collapsed the list, and the Click opened a different Collapsible that the collapse moved under the pointer (`settings-advanced-session-summary` / `settings-advanced-catalog-refresh`), 3 of 3 runs. That is how Textual 8.2.8's Pilot works: `_post_mouse_events` forwards MouseDown, MouseUp and Click one at a time with a pause between, and the screen hit-tests each again at forward time, so its Click goes to whatever the offset holds after the collapse. A terminal never sends a Click: the driver posts MouseDown and MouseUp, and `App.on_event` makes a Click only when the release lands on the same widget as the press, delivering it at once to that widget. Posted that way (the same press and release through `App.post_message`), the pressed control was focused and nothing else toggled at every hold measured (0, 10, 150 and 400 ms between press and release). A release before the collapse reached the screen opened the pressed title; a release after it activated nothing ("or none" in AC#1): the click that dismisses the list is then only a focus move. Making that held press activate the pressed control would need the picker to hold its collapse until the release, which Textual exposes no event for; left as is, for the controller.

Test (Tests/UI/test_settings_default_model_picker.py, gated whole in the UI PR census, private profile): `test_a_click_below_an_open_list_lands_on_the_pressed_control_or_none[released-at-once|held]-[model|provider]` opens the Default model list or the Provider list with keys, presses Generation defaults' title with the driver's MouseDown and releases it at once or after the collapse has reached the screen (asserting another control is under the pointer by then), with no Escape. It asserts focus is on the pressed title, that no other Collapsible toggled, and, held, that none did. 4 passed. Locally the released-at-once cases opened the pressed title every time (a temporary strict assertion, 4 of 4), but the test accepts "or none" there, as AC#1 does, since a slow runner can render the collapse between the two posts. Negative control: swapping the two posts for `pilot.click` fails all four cases (`{'settings-advanced-catalog-refresh'}` toggled).

The Switch model search has no collapsing list (its pair list is always shown), so it has no such click. AC#2 was already ticked (moot since TASK-33006.2).
