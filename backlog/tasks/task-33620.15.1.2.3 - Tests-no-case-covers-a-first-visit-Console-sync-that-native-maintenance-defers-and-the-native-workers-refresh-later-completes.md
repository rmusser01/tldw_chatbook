Found in the pre-merge review of TASK-33620.15.1.2 (2026-10-27). Tests/UI/test_console_attach_completion.py pins that a deferred refresh (maintenance, replay, coalesced) does not finish attachment, but no case covers a first visit whose first full sync is deferred by the native maintenance probe and is then completed by the native worker's own refresh. Pin that interaction so a later change to either path cannot leave a first visit unreconciled or reconciled twice.
