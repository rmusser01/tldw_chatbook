## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found by TASK-33620.15.47's paired live rounds (2026-11-05, origin/dev 29592ecc9b against its branch dcb5017efb, Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 via the review proxy, 160x45, a fresh isolated profile per launch, load1 96-128 from a bounded busy-loop generator, 35 sends per arm). TASK-33620.15.47 made storage admission's path resolution one kernel walk: resolution per entry fell from 0.31 to 0.13 ms (median wall) and no longer held a post-commit step of 50 ms or more. But run start's UI-loop transaction entries still reached 10 ms or more as often as on dev (35 of 423 entries on the branch, 36 of 417 on dev), and the post-commit step's entries reached 10 ms in 3 of 35 sends (dev: 2 of 35). None of the branch's step waits was mostly path resolution. One was the BEGIN statement (26.7 ms on 0.3 ms of CPU), one was evidence re-observation (_Evidence.observe, 11.4 ms), and one was 39.1 ms in the rest of admission's entry code, beside 10.8 ms of resolution. Without any resolution at all, each of the three would still have reached 10 ms. Entry CPU is low (median 0.57 ms), so these are waits for the CPU or the GIL (lessons-live-verification, TASK-33620.15.21), and they land roughly in proportion to an entry's CPU and GIL releases. A warm entry still makes 49 filesystem calls (87 for a bound profile), 30 (68) of them evidence stamps. Storage admission's guarantees are set by backlog/decisions/126-complete-local-backup-and-recovery.md; a fix keeps them. Evidence is outside the repo, in the measuring session's directory: ~/.claude/projects/-Users-macbook-dev-Documents-GitHub-tldw-chatbook/c28a6a75-4221-4434-989d-fbf24a73c96b/tools/evidence/bd5/R47/ (p1, p3, all_sends.json).

Owner decision, 2026-11-05: TASK-33620.15.47 closed on its AC #2's first clause (no post-commit step held by path resolution). Its former second clause (the step's entries reach 10 ms or more in at most half as many sends as on origin/dev) is this task's AC #2, together with the remaining entry waits: BEGIN, evidence re-observation and the rest of admission's entry code.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
**Status: left In Progress for review.** AC #1, #3 and #4 are met and ticked. AC #2 is met on its letter, but on counts too small to separate the arms (details below), so its box is left for the reviewer. The measurement moved the fix out of storage admission. ADR-125's helper launch changes, and the owner must accept that change's descriptor posture (the ADR-125 note dated 2026-11-20).

**Measured first (AC #1).** Setup: origin/dev d85e90bc9e, the real app on a fresh isolated profile per launch, Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 through the review proxy, 160x45, 4 sends per launch, and a bounded 88-child busy-loop generator. The probe split every UI-loop ChaChaNotes transaction entry in run start into path resolution, evidence re-observation, BEGIN and the rest of admission (statvfs, other `os` calls, other SQL, Python). It also timed every `os` call in the entry and snapshotted every other thread's stack after any wait of 2 ms or more. Its sampler and heartbeat threads were off. The R47 kit had replaced the connection's `execute` override with the C base method, which bypassed the tracked cursor; this probe wraps the override.

Per entry, dev at load1 105-150 (36 paired sends, 430 entries):

| piece | wall, median (p90) | CPU, median (p90) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| whole entry | 0.60 (1.64) ms | 0.59 (1.09) ms |
| path resolution | 0.15 (0.48) ms | 0.15 (0.27) ms |
| evidence re-observation | 0.19 (0.36) ms | 0.19 (0.34) ms |
| BEGIN | 0.015 (0.06) ms | 0.015 (0.06) ms |
| rest of admission | 0.24 (0.47) ms | 0.23 (0.42) ms |

A warm entry makes 55 `os` calls and 3 statements. About 12 entries run per send: `list_bindings` x6, `load_applicable_branch_memory`, `library_activity_snapshot`, `_read_inline` and the post-commit step's two (`_build_durable_trace_request`, `write_trajectory_rows`).

Across all three dev high-load rounds (48 sends, 573 entries), 27 entries took 10 ms or more. By the piece with the largest wall, they were held by:

| piece | entries | how they waited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| path resolution | 13 | single `os.open` / `os.stat` calls |
| evidence re-observation | 9 | one `lstat` of 92.9 ms |
| rest | 4 | |
| BEGIN | 1 | 14.1 ms |

The holding piece took a median 90% of the entry, on a median 1.4 ms of entry CPU. In each case the wait sat on a single call wherever the main thread released the GIL.

**What held the waits.** Not admission's cost:
- Of the 27 dev entries of 10 ms or more, 21 overlapped a private-SQLite helper launch (`Popen._execute_child`) on another thread, against 11-14% of random 10 ms intervals in the same send windows. Every one of the calibration round's 8 did.
- A send launches 25-55 helpers between Enter and the POST, one per worker-thread connection (`run_owned_db_call` retires each one).
- On macOS a `close_fds=True` child is forked, and CPython holds the GIL for the whole fork; it releases it only around `vfork`, which is Linux-only. Helper launches took a median 10.5-12 ms at load1 110-150.
- Off the app, in a 600 MB process at low load: a forked launch took 6.5 ms and stalled the main thread for 5 ms or more in 285 of 286 launches. A `posix_spawn` launch took 0.38 ms and stalled it for 1 ms or more in 0 of 336. `os.posix_spawn` also holds the GIL, but only for its syscall.
- Any admission-side cut (fewer stamps, deduplicated checks) would only have moved the same GIL handoff to the next release in the callback.

**Change.**
- `private_sqlite_process.HelperLease.start` passes `close_fds=not _SPAWN_WITHOUT_FORK` (True on Linux, False on macOS). `close_fds=False` is what selects `subprocess`'s `posix_spawn` path. Linux keeps its vfork launch, which already releases the GIL; Windows launches no helper.
- `private_sqlite_helper_entry` now closes every descriptor above 2 that it inherited, before any other statement (under `__main__`, silently exiting 1 on failure). Python and SQLite open their descriptors close-on-exec (checked), so this normally closes nothing. Either way the helper works with exactly the three pipes the fork gave it.
- Storage admission is not touched: no stamp, check, refusal, lock order or thread changes.
- ADR-125 has a dated note marked pending owner review.
