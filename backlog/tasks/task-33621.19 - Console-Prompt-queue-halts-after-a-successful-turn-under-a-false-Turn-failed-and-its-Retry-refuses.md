## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
**Impact.** With two or more prompts queued in one persisted Console tab, the queue sends at most one of them and then stops. The shelf changes to 'Queue 2/10 · Turn failed · Next: …' with [Mana] [Retry] about 3 s after the first queued turn is durably committed, while that turn is still generating. Every turn completes normally: the log shows `controller_submit status=completed` and no errors. More than 40 s later the remaining prompts have still not been sent. The tab reads 'Q2' and the header reads 'Ready'.

The recovery controls do not help:
- Retry and the modal's 'Retry failed' both reply 'No matching stopped or failed turn is available.'
- Only 'Skip & resume' works. It skips nothing, and each press advances exactly one entry before the queue pauses again.
- After every turn has succeeded, the rail row still shows a failure glyph.
- The Manage modal has 13 buttons, 7 of them disabled with no reason. 'Paused' is a disabled button used as a status. Rows are numbered from 0.

The cause: `settle_durable_acceptance` (`console_prompt_queue.py:1110-1113`) sets PAUSED/FAILED whenever entries are still waiting, and the live post-commit acknowledgement calls it for every queued turn. The next `claim_next` is then refused, and `_pause_after_exception` pauses the queue again as FAILED. The coordinator's `_SUCCESS` check is not the cause, because the terminal status is COMPLETED.

**Severity / prior art.** P1, REGRESSION of Done tasks:
- TASK-14805: AC#1 drains separate FIFO turns in one chain, AC#4 Retry/Resume semantics, AC#8 joined controller tests prove ordered multi-turn drains.
- TASK-14808: AC#2 the shelf and manager expose pause/resume/retry.

Also ADR-098 (lines 71-75) and TASK-31993.13. GAP1-04 and GAP5-09 are two independent reports of this mechanism.

**Suggested direction.**
- When a live chain owns the exact claim, settle it without pausing. Keep the FAILED pause only for the detached, no-chain case.
- Offer Resume rather than Retry when no failed message exists, or store the failed message id on the pause.
- Rename 'Skip & resume'.
- Number rows from 1, show the queue state as text, and hide or explain actions that don't apply.
- Check `is_mounted` in the modal's worker completion before applying feedback.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] #2 The shelf shows 'Turn failed' only when a queued turn's message actually failed, and it names that turn; Retry then re-runs it
- [ ] #3 When the queue is paused with no failed message, the offered primary action is Resume and it resumes draining; no offered queue action replies 'No matching stopped or failed turn is available.'
- [ ] #4 After all queued turns succeed, no tab, rail row or shelf shows a failure glyph, 'Turn failed' or 'Prompt queue paused'
- [ ] #5 The Manage modal numbers entries from 1, shows the queue state as text rather than as a disabled button, hides or explains actions that don't apply, and no action label suggests discarding a prompt it does not discard
- [ ] #6 A joined controller test drives at least three queued entries through the real durable post-commit acknowledgement path with a live chain, asserts that all of them drain, and fails on the pre-fix code
