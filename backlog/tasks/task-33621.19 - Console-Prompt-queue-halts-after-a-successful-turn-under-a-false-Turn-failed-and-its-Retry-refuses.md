## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
**Impact.** With two or more prompts queued in one persisted Console tab, the queue sends at most one of them and then stops. The shelf changes to 'Queue 2/10 · Turn failed · Next: …' with [Mana] [Retry] about 3 s after the first queued turn is durably committed, while that turn is still generating. Every turn completes normally: the log shows `controller_submit status=completed` and no errors. More than 40 s later the remaining prompts have still not been sent. The tab reads 'Q2' and the header reads 'Ready'.

The recovery controls do not help:
- Retry and the modal's 'Retry failed' both reply 'No matching stopped or failed turn is available.'
- Only 'Skip & resume' works. It skips nothing, and each press advances exactly one entry before the queue pauses again.
- After every turn has succeeded, the rail row still shows a failure glyph.
- The Manage modal has 13 buttons, 7 of them disabled with no reason. 'Paused' is a disabled button used as a status. Rows are numbered from 0.

The cause: `settle_durable_acceptance` (`console_prompt_queue.py:1110-1113`) sets PAUSED/FAILED whenever entries are still waiting, and the live post-commit acknowledgement calls it for every queued turn. The next `claim_next` is then refused, and `_pause_after_exception` pauses the queue again as FAILED. The coordinator's `_SUCCESS` check is not the cause, because the terminal status is COMPLETED.

**Severity / prior art.** P1, REGRESSION of Done tasks:
- TASK-14805: AC#1 drains separate FIFO turns in one chain, AC#4 Retry/Resume semantics, AC#8 joined controller tests prove ordered multi-turn drains.
- TASK-14808: AC#2 the shelf and manager expose pause/resume/retry.

Also ADR-098 (lines 71-75) and TASK-31993.13. GAP1-04 and GAP5-09 are two independent reports of this mechanism.

**Suggested direction.**
- When a live chain owns the exact claim, settle it without pausing. Keep the FAILED pause only for the detached, no-chain case.
- Offer Resume rather than Retry when no failed message exists, or store the failed message id on the pause.
- Rename 'Skip & resume'.
- Number rows from 1, show the queue state as text, and hide or explain actions that don't apply.
- Check `is_mounted` in the modal's worker completion before applying feedback.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
A durable post-commit queue acknowledgement no longer pauses a queue that its live chain is still draining.

**Cause:** `settle_durable_acceptance` set PAUSED/FAILED and released the slot whenever prompts were waiting. The durable `queue_acknowledgement` effect calls it for every queued turn in a persisted chat, so every multi-entry queue halted after one successful turn under a false "Turn failed" whose Retry could only refuse.

**Approach:**
- `settle_durable_acceptance` gains `live_chain_owns_claim`. When the coordinator's chain owns the exact claim, the claim is settled like `settle_claim`: mode and reservation stay with the chain. The detached (no chain) case still pauses.
- `acknowledge_durable_acceptance` keeps the live owner's `current_entry_id`, as the ephemeral `turn_accepted` path does. The drain loop, or `finish_recovered_entry` for a reclaimed Library-preparation or persistence retry/bypass, still advances or finishes the chain. Clearing it had left reclaimed durable chains DRAINING/HELD with no driver; review caught that.
- `ConsolePromptQueueUIController.recovery_turn()` names the exact retry target: the newest assistant message, if failed or stopped, plus a bounded preview of its prompt.
  - The shelf shows `Turn failed: "<prompt>"` with Retry only when that target exists. Otherwise a FAILED pause reads Paused with Resume.
  - Retry and "Retry stopped" re-run exactly that message.
  - A `-narrow` shelf ellipsizes the summary so Retry stays on-screen at 80 columns.
- The Manage modal:
  - numbers rows from 1 and shows the state as text;
  - hides actions that don't apply and gives disabled ones tooltips;
  - renames "Skip & resume" to "Resume next" and puts the retry target in its render key (it re-derives on each 0.2 s poll);
  - makes post-dismiss feedback a no-op;
  - runs Resume and Retry as app-owned workers, so closing the modal doesn't cancel the queued turn.
- The shelf's primary actions run in a non-exclusive app-owned worker group, so Pause after Resume no longer cancels the in-flight queued turn.

**Decisions:** the retry target is derived from the transcript rather than stored on the pause, which avoids a registry schema change. `chat_screen.py` stays at net +0 lines (25496).
