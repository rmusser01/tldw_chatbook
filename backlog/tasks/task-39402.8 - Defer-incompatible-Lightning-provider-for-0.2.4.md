## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES:BEGIN -->

0.2.4 removes Lightning from public extras, discovery, defaults and install guidance. The shared release refusal recognizes both retained identities and is checked before transcription, capture and worker effects. Local provider identities and the native IPC vocabulary stay intact. Meetings honor an installed explicit choice independently of the global selection; an unavailable exact choice does not fall back.

Dictation retains the exact saved value, including the older alias, and explains the refusal. A deliberate choice in a fresh deferred view uses the existing batched settings save and starts no service. Passive control updates and unrelated privacy edits preserve the saved provider. A view that already owns a service asks to close and reopen before replacing it; the view keeps that service, and terminal retirement stays one-way.

[ADR-025](../decisions/025-shared-stt-artifacts-and-runtime-routing.md) records the deferral. [ADR-335](../decisions/335-offline-tiktoken-runtime-assets.md) and [ADR-318](../decisions/318-vendored-chunking-engine-parity.md) keep the tokenizer dependency, assets and cache authority unchanged. Under [ADR-097](../decisions/097-boot-budget-ratchets.md), Speech Lab imports Dictation only when mounting that view. The original cold check went from 426,679 lines against its 426,575 limit to 422,618; the first-send guard stayed at 93 of 94 modules. The existing Speech Lab configuration snapshot is unchanged.

The final ratchet, mounted choice, persistence, retirement, button delivery and facade forwarding checks passed 30/30. Two Console warmup repeat assertions reproduce the exact initial 068-Chat-25 failures and remain open outside this change. All 19 tokenizer security checks passed. The parsed package metadata differs only by the Lightning extra and its two dependency edges, and Ruff reported no new findings. The canonical release artifact build and installation remain under TASK-39402.3. No real speech model, capture device or upstream dependency resolution was qualified here.
