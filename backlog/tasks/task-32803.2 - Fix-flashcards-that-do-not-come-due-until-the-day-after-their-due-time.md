## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->

A card reviewed at 09:00 with a one-day interval does not reappear until 00:00 UTC two days later. The study-statistics window drops up to a day of reviews the same way. Reproduced: a card due one hour ago returns a due count of zero, and the same instant in the column's own shape returns one.
