## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found while measuring TASK-33628.5 (dev 7d155170dc, ConsoleHarness + file-backed ChaChaNotes DB, 160x48). Selecting message 1 of a 1,000-message chain mounts all 1,000 rows (8,085 widgets under #console-transcript-region) and the app takes 54 s to settle; at 3,000 messages it is 24,085 widgets and 183 s. With those rows mounted, one toggle of the console-transcript-region-focused class (frame.sync_console_focus_paint, run on every DescendantFocus/Blur) costs 9.6-12.2 s at 1k and 29-37 s at 3k, almost all in textual_css_fastpath.apply restyling every descendant. After the 1k selection had settled, a Delete of that subtree still held the loop for up to 2.6 s (10.2 s busy in total), and Undo -- which reselects the restored root, re-mounting the rows -- held it for up to 17.4 s (56.7 s busy). The equivalent 3k run hit the 300 s pytest timeout before its Delete finished (the selection settle and two class toggles took about 250 s of it). The durable write TASK-33628.5 moved off the loop was 0.15-1.8 s of this. Probe numbers and the stack samples are in TASK-33628.5's implementation notes.
