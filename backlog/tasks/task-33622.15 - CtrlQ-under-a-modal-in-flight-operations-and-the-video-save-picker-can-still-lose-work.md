## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Residual gaps found by the PR #2949 (TASK-33622.10) round-6 review. #2949 made Ctrl+Q a priority binding and gave every modal that guards its own close a confirm_quit. These cases remain:
- Modals that REFUSE to close while an operation is in flight have no confirm_quit, so Ctrl+Q quits past the operation: ConsoleForkChatModal while committing (console_fork_chat_modal.py ~406), ConsoleCapturePolicyDialog and ConsoleTracePrivacyDialog while applying (~809, ~1038), and ProjectSkillsImportModal mid-import (~276).
- While the generated video's Save-to-disk picker (EnhancedFileSave) or its Replace / Destination-changed confirmation is open, the capacity modal is already dismissed (UI/Console_Modules/video.py ~958-978). The quit walk finds no hook and the video is lost without asking. This matches today's design, where cancelling the picker also discards silently, so it needs an owner decision.
- Interview race: if Leave > Discard is already running, Ctrl+Q still asks 'Discard interview and quit?'. If the discard finishes under the quit prompt, the refused dismiss_safe_once is never retried, and after Continue the screen stays open and busy over a discarded draft.
Every review round on #2949 found more modal paths, so the durable fix is a census rather than one more modal.
