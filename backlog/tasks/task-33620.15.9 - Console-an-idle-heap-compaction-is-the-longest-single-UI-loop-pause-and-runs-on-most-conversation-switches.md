## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
TASK-33620.15.3 moved main-thread generation-2 collection out of Console send windows by re-freezing the long-lived heap at idle points, with a compaction (unfreeze, full collection, refreeze) bounding frozen garbage. In its live round 9 (2026-10-20, against origin/dev 2d071d6d71) the branch's five worst UI-loop pauses were all compactions: 492/611 ms CPU/wall at worst and 296 ms CPU median, against dev's worst automatic generation-2 pass of 202/212 ms. There were 27 compactions over 24 conversation switches and 20 runs, because a switch away from a transcript frozen while shown owes one. A compaction starts only after 5 s without input and with nothing in flight, but once started it cannot be interrupted, so a key pressed during it waits up to about 0.6 s. No acceptance criterion of TASK-33620.15.3 measured that delay. Owner sign-off is needed on whether this trade-off stands or the compaction cadence changes.
