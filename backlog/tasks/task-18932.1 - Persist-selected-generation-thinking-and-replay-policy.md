## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Create the bounded thinking-envelope and conversation replay-policy foundation for Console Assistant generations. Persist the selected answer and its displayable or content-free proprietary evidence together, keep ADR-063 continuation separate, preserve unknown durable versions on unrelated writes, and carry the supported fields through local persistence, dispatch recovery, sync, conflicts, and versioned backend capability checks.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 A bounded, versioned thinking-envelope parser accepts only ordered displayable or structurally text-free proprietary blocks, emits content-free diagnostics, and preserves unsupported durable versions opaquely while imports/incoming sync reject them.
- [ ] #2 ChaChaNotes adds nullable `messages.thinking_blocks_json` and nullable `conversations.thinking_history_policy`; existing rows receive no synthesized evidence and missing policy resolves to Auto.
- [ ] #3 Normal assistant creation, terminal update, stop/failure settlement, dispatch recovery, hydration, deletion, and generic unrelated content writes preserve or clear thinking exactly according to the approved generation-ownership rules.
- [ ] #4 Every live Console variant and regeneration snapshot owns answer, thinking, usage, and separate continuation/provenance state; selecting or abandoning a variant cannot pair one attempt's answer with another attempt's thinking or continuation.
- [ ] #5 The selected answer, terminal thinking envelope, and continuation projection update as one durable generation projection; sync payloads, hashes, conflict handling, and tombstones treat them as one whole-record owner without claiming a cross-database transaction.
- [ ] #6 Conversation replay policy round-trips through conversation persistence and existing conversation synchronization surfaces, while the device-local display preference remains absent from those records.
- [ ] #7 Persistent adapters advertise a versioned thinking round-trip capability; an opted-in thinking-capable persistent path without required support fails locally before any provider contact, while unaffected legacy/non-thinking and ephemeral paths retain compatibility.
- [ ] #8 Genuine historical migration, envelope mutation/negative, variant, stopped/failed, preservation, sync-owner, conflict, and backend-capability tests pass without pinning a provisional final schema number.
<!-- AC:END -->
