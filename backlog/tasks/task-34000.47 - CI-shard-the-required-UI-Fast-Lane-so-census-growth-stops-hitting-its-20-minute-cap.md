## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Why: the `ui-fast-lane` job is part of the required "Derived artifacts reproduce from their sources" check. It runs every census-listed `Tests/UI` file serially under a 20-minute timeout, and if it times out the required check fails for every PR. On 2026-10-03, successful dev-based PR runs took 13.6–17.2 minutes of job wall time, leaving 3–6 minutes of headroom. One branch that grew the census (`codex/console-tool-ux-followups`) ran 19.7–19.9 minutes and timed out twice (runs 37134955509 and 37106658176).

Every new real-app Pilot pin the review programme adds makes this worse. TASK-34000.1 had to split its quit-guard file and keep only a lean core in the gate to stay inside the budget. The durable fix is to split the lane, not to keep trimming tests.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
