## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Why: Seen live by the Task 10 implementer (TASK-34000.40 N-34, 60-file import walk) and recorded as pre-existing. With notes of about 1 MB, the app log carried `event_loop_stall` entries of 4-5 s inside `Notes/note_import_plan_models.py` `embedded_file_count` (:158, a scan with `EMBED_SCAN` :146 over each body), reached through `item_embedded_file_count` (:1075) and `_embed_clause` in `Library/library_note_import_state.py` (:1175-1191). While the loop is blocked nothing repaints and no key is read; progress and receipt painting lagged by more than 10 s at machine load 40-70. With 4 KB notes the same walk was responsive. This is one live observation under heavy load; the stall was not isolated in a benchmark, and whether the scan is run once or per repaint was not established.
