## Description

Found by TASK-34100.3's review round v5r1 live verification (finding G3BV5-V6), outside that task's diff, and reproduced on 2026-11-13. Moonshot's `/v1/models` lists six models (`kimi-k2.6`, `kimi-k2.7-code`, `kimi-k2.7-code-highspeed`, `kimi-k3`, `kimi-k3.1-pro`, `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed`), and the setup wizard's Model step offers them. A user who picked a model in the wizard therefore cannot find its siblings in the picker.
