## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Since TASK-33620.1.12 an in-flight run's tool calls are refused while the emergency stop is active. A scheduled agent run that was dispatched before the stop landed therefore completes "done" (or ends after two held turns) with its tool calls refused: its work is skipped, and nothing retries it once the stop is cleared. Scheduled dispatches that were still queued at the stop are held and fire after Clear, so the two cases disagree. Found in the TASK-33620.1.12 review.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->

- [ ] #3 Covered by a test that drives a real scheduled dispatch through a stop and a Clear
