## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Make every guarded transition go through one dialog backed by per-domain save and discard executors, and fence item loads so a late or failed load never shows the wrong item. Safety work with no layout change. Spec §5.2 "B5b-1 · Guard, executors and the load fence (no layout change)"; see also §3.12 "ADR-046 draft veto: one predicate, one dialog, the complete trigger table", §4.11 "State and memory" and §5.9 G4 (guard). Size M. Fixes RP-037 and RP-030 (item memory); hardens RP-078 and RP-079.

Parent: TASK-33910 (Roleplay on the Library frame, sub-project B). Spec: `Docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-roleplay-library-frame-design.md` (PR #2960).

**Scope**
- `_run_guarded` routed through `roleplay_has_unsaved_work()` and the per-domain save and discard executors (§3.12), with one dialog (Save and continue · Discard and continue · Stay) and `RoleplayDraftRecoveryDialog` for a failed save. The executors replace `_save_aggregate_roleplay_drafts` and its discard branch.
- The §3.12 rows for Duplicate, same-kind New…, Import…, Revert… and the two Delete cases; the §4.11 Delete split.
- The generation fence, with the load-failure state `couldn't open · Retry` and the `ConflictError` copy "Changed elsewhere · Reload".
- `ItemMemory` (entry anchor and conversation id only; no view or focus memory).
- The visit-lifecycle resume duty: re-fetch the loaded record by id and version.
- No layout change, so no screenshot round (§5.4 item 6).

**Interim (§5.3):** the state words `couldn't open · Retry` and "Changed elsewhere · Reload" show on the Inspector's selection line until B5b-2's title row.

**Prerequisites:** sub-project A TASK-33791 (RP-078/079) must be on dev (§5.5, H); TASK-33790 (RP-075) should be (S).

**Slice gates (card):** pre-import +0; the `personas_screen.py` row measured and lowered; ADR-011 capture (the resume re-fetch and the fenced load path; retires `_save_aggregate_roleplay_drafts`).

**Ordering:** after B5a; needs B3's form executors under the guard (§5.1).
