## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found by TASK-33620.15.21's live rounds (2026-10-30, origin/dev 7536ac3b87, Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 via the review proxy, 160x45, a fresh isolated profile per launch, host load1 99-127 from a bounded busy-loop generator). At that load, run start's longest UI-loop callback had a median of 132 ms wall on first sends and 119 ms on later sends, with about 11 ms of its own CPU (48 sends). At load 3-9 it was 16.8 and 7.0 ms (24 sends). The post-commit step was that callback in only 6 of the 48 high-load sends.

Exact timers on 32 of the high-load sends show which callback it usually is. In 14 of the 32, most of it was the catalog fingerprint's MCP source check: UnifiedMCPControlPlaneService.run_catalog_fingerprint > LocalMCPControlService.catalog_fingerprint > the mcp_source_participants guard > binding > MCP/recovery_activation.selected_path. That check does a storage admission plus generation witnesses, the stat and read syscalls in storage_admission's _Evidence.observe (_posture, _content).
- First sends: 6 of 8. The callback's median was 209 ms wall and 12 ms CPU, and the check alone took 199 ms.
- Later sends: 8 of 24. The callback's median was 113 ms, and the check took 107 ms.
- At low load the same check takes 5.4 ms (first sends) and 3.9 ms (later sends).

The check's CPU about doubles at load, to 8 ms. Most of its wall time is spent waiting to be scheduled again after its syscalls release the GIL (see lessons-live-verification, TASK-33620.15.1.6).

TASK-33620.15.5 kept the fingerprint's two store reads on the UI loop and split them across loop turns. The reason was that TASK-33620.15.1's worker hop made first sends reach the provider 97-249 ms later. A fix has to weigh that cost at both loads.

Other run-start holds at that load are smaller or rarer:
- Provider entry's response-rule bindings read (ResponseRuleRuntime.begin_generation): median 5 ms, up to 123 ms.
- Callbacks with 2-4 ms of CPU and 60-300 ms of wall, where no code dominates.

Evidence: tools/evidence/bd5/M21/ (rounds r1-r3, the kit, analysis_all.txt).
