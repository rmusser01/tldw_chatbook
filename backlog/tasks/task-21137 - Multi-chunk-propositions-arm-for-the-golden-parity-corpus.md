## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
From TASK-21136's Task-2 review: the seven propositions fixtures that joined the golden-parity corpus (`Tests/Chunking/golden/`, generated by `generate_golden.py`, asserted by `test_golden_parity.py`) are all single-chunk — the generator's frozen grid (corpus × `max_size=50`, `overlap=10`) packs by proposition COUNT for this method, so every small corpus in the grid yields exactly one chunk. The corpus is the drift detector for the vendored engine, so a propositions change that only altered multi-chunk packing (proposition-count boundaries) would regenerate identical fixtures and pass. Close the gap with one or a few longer-prose arms whose propositions output packs into ≥2 chunks — via a new corpus entry or a method-specific arm — without re-gridding in a way that rewrites the existing 77 fixtures (Task 2's accepted deviation documented why in-place special-casing of the shared options was not taken).
