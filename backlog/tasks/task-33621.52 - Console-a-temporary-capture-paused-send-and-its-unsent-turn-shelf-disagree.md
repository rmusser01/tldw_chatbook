## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
**Impact.** A send in a temporary chat with trace capture on pauses as TEMPORARY_CAPTURE. Like a Library pause, it then shows on two surfaces: the transcript card (Save & Send / Send without capture / Cancel) and the unsent-turn shelf (Restore / Discard). TASK-33621.20 made the two agree for Library and re-paused sends only. For a temporary-capture pause they still disagree, per the TASK-33621.20 pre-merge re-review (found by reading the code; reproduce before fixing):
- **Shelf Discard does nothing to the send.** The shelf entry goes, but the paused preparation stays, so the next send in that chat is refused ("Last send is blocked; resolve it first") while the shelf no longer shows why.
- **The shelf can offer an already-sent message.** After Save & Send or Send without capture, the card does not hold the turn off the shelf or drop it, so once the send settles the shelf offers Restore for a message that was sent. Restoring it sends it twice.

**Cause.** One rule does two jobs. "The card holds or forgets the shelf entry" and "the shelf may release this pause" both key off `SHELF_RELEASABLE_PAUSES` (retrieval, destination changed, persistence), and TEMPORARY_CAPTURE is in neither.

**Suggested direction.** Split the two rules: which pauses a card action holds and settles on the shelf, and which pauses a shelf Discard or Restore may cancel. Then add TEMPORARY_CAPTURE to both, with its own test, on the real controller and screen.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
