## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found while measuring TASK-33620.15.14 (live, 2026-10-20, origin/dev 2d071d6d71 and the TASK-33620.15.5 branch, 160x45, established isolated profiles). After launch the Console's first frame shows with the nav bar's Home button focused; the composer takes focus only when ChatScreen._restore_console_workbench_focus runs from the screen's idle callback, 0.1 to 1.9 s after that first frame (median about 1 s over 44 launches). Keys that reach the app before then are forwarded to the Home button, bubble to ChatScreen.on_key, fail _should_capture_console_input (focus is outside the composer) and are dropped with no cue. A 29-character prompt typed 0.5 s after the first frame was lost whole in 3 of 10 launches on fresh profiles on origin/dev (3 of 10 on the TASK-33620.15.5 branch, the same; 6 of 44 on dev over all batches), while a single key at 0.5 s always landed in 32 launches because it waited in the queue past the focus restore. The first batches, on profiles reused across launches, looked arm-dependent (an arm whose bursts had landed carried a restored draft into its later launches; one whose bursts were lost did not); on fresh profiles the two arms matched. A user who starts typing as soon as the Console appears can lose what they typed.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Seen again 2026-10-20 (TASK-33620.15.5 review smoke): on a new launcher 'ready' profile's first launch, a 33-34 character prompt typed 12 s after the composer first rendered, with no click, was dropped on both arms (origin/dev 906497d9b4 and the TASK-33620.15.5 branch, one launch each); after a click on the composer it landed and sent. So on a first launch the composer may stay unfocused far longer than the 0.1-1.9 s measured above. Evidence: scratchpad/uxrev/evidence/bd5/RS2/smoke-review/ (01 branch, 03 dev).

**2026-10-27 fix (branch fix/task-33620.15.17-keys-before-first-focus).**

Measured first (AC #1), live on origin/dev (3be493413a, then a2b7104d1e), 160x45, a fresh seeded profile per launch (setup complete, model-catalog consent recorded so no modal covers the Console), real Anthropic claude-haiku-4-5 through the review proxy, a `sitecustomize` probe on PYTHONPATH (Screen.set_focus, App._display, ChatScreen.on_key / _restore_console_workbench_focus / on_screen_resume):
- Every launch's first ChatScreen frame shows `NavigationButton#nav-home` focused: Textual's AUTO_FOCUS pick, set by `push_screen` ~0.3 s before that frame.
- The composer takes focus only when the activation restore runs from the screen's idle callbacks after the attach reconcile (its first `_sync_native_console_chat_ui`), and its deferred `focus()` lands: 0.21-2.52 s after the first frame (median 1.53 s, 10 dev launches; 1.88-2.34 s in 4 more).
- A 29-character prompt typed 0.3 s after the first frame was lost whole in 7 of 10 dev launches: all 29 keys reached `ChatScreen.on_key` with Home focused and failed `_should_capture_console_input`. It landed only when the busy loop held the keys in its queue past the restore. Typed 0.05 s after the first frame: lost in 4 of 4.

Fix (`UI/Console_Modules/composer_run_controls.py`):
- `arm_arrival_focus`, called from a new `@on(ScreenResume)` handler on ChatScreen, remembers the focus a real activation found when it sits outside every Console pane, and watches `Screen.focused`; the first focus change ends the window. The attach reconcile replays `on_screen_resume` directly, so that replay never arms (a focus the user moved by then stays theirs). The first version armed from inside `on_screen_resume`; dev's `test_persistent_attach_sync_failure_has_bounded_backoff_and_resume_retry` calls it on a `ChatScreen.__new__` stub, whose `focused` reactive raises, and went red only on the branch.
- `claim_arrival_key`, called in `ChatScreen.on_key` just before the capture check: a printable key typed while that focus still holds focuses the draft synchronously (`focus_draft_from`) when the restore would pick the composer. That key and the ones already queued behind it are then typed into the draft by the normal routing, in order.
- Unchanged input owners (AC #3): a focus the user moved (watcher), an explicit Resume (`_resume_navigation_startup_in_progress`), a collapsed composer, the setup card (read from `_build_console_setup_card_state`, so also before the first guidance sync has shown it; on_key already returns while it is showing), a modal (receives the keys itself). Non-printable keys (Enter, chords, editing keys) are never claimed.
- `workbench_restore_target` now holds the restore's fallback-pane order (last pane, composer, transcript), shared by `_restore_console_workbench_focus` and the claim.
- Not covered: a warm revisit whose screen remembers no focus at all gets Textual's auto pick after the arm runs; its restore there is not reconcile-gated and lands at the next idle.

Tests (`Tests/UI/test_console_arrival_focus_keys.py`): four private-profile cases on a harness that holds the activation restore (the window, made deterministic) and types through the app's driver queue (`pump_probe.key`), plus 12 unit gates.
- Red on the pre-fix code for the right reason: the in-order burst case and the modal-return case ("draft '', focus NavigationButton#nav-home"); the unit gates (no `claim_arrival_key` or `arm_arrival_focus`).
- Guards that hold on dev too (moved focus, collapsed composer) went red under a mutation that disabled the watcher (both mounted guards: "'name one colourx'", "'y' == ''").
- One node in the UI PR lane census (floor 908 -> 909; after the rebase, dev's 964 + 1 = 965).
