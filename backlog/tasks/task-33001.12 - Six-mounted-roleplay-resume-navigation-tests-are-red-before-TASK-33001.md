Rider from TASK-33001.6 (review fix round 1). TASK-33001.6 edited pins in `Tests/UI/test_console_roleplay_resume_navigation.py`, and none of them can go green locally.

**What fails.**
- Unwrapped, 9 of the 19 tests fail in setup with ADR-126 `RecoveryRequired`, because the file does not use `private_profile_test`, and a 10th fails on `_MessagePump__parent`.
- With every module-level test wrapped, 6 still fail:
  - identically at program base 144329dee0, at fb8fc1854d and at c902f34b69;
  - the per-test failure reasons at fb8fc1854d and c902f34b69 differ only by the removed 'provider-intent' event and a ContextVar address.

**Causes found.** Three tests (`test_mounted_resume_orders_consumers_once_and_suppresses_competitors`, `..._settles_first_chat_once_without_intermediate_presentation` and `..._releases_transient_first_chat_without_rollback_focus`) record a leading 'intermediate-native-sync' event.
- A stack captured at that call shows where it comes from: `ChatScreen.on_mount` → `call_after_refresh(_reconcile_console_after_attach)` → `_sync_native_console_chat_ui()`.
- This runs before the resume worker's first consumer. The attach reconcile landed in b7dd8e53f2.
- In a scratch copy, presetting `screen._console_attach_sync_complete = True` makes the ordering test pass fully, both at c902f34b69 and at fb8fc1854d.
- So the pins are otherwise right. The open question is whether the attach-time sync is an intermediate presentation that the resume contract forbids (a product fix) or whether the pins are stale (a test fix).

**Not traced.**
- `test_mounted_global_resume_outranks_registry_active_workspace`: `assert 2 == 1`.
- `test_mounted_missing_resume_falls_back_to_registry_active_workspace`: `assert 'global' == 'workspace-startup'`.
- `test_resume_navigation_continues_after_chat_handoff_release`: `LookupError` on the `active_app` ContextVar, on a bare `ChatScreen.__new__` instance.

**Fixed, then broken again on dev (Phase 9 check, 2026-10-18).** 3146bbd8da (#3011) fixed this rider: it wrapped the mounted tests in `private_profile_test` and made `_reconcile_console_after_attach` skip the attach-time sync during an explicit Resume. The rider stays open, and its acceptance criteria stand as written.

**The blocking-modal focus flake, traced (2026-10-18).** `test_mounted_no_resume_keeps_blocking_modal_focus_transfer` was a test race, not a product defect. Stacks recorded at each card sync and each `focus_primary_action` name what moved the focus: the 0.25 s credential poll, `_poll_console_credential_readiness`. Its first pass, and its pass once the background Claude credential read settles, sync the control bar, which syncs the setup card; `_apply_console_setup_block` then pulls the forced composer focus back onto `#console-setup-modal-action`, as designed. In 16 of 20 instrumented runs that first pass came 36-147 ms after the test's `composer.focus()`, so the test passed only when its one `pilot.pause()` returned first. In 2 more, the selector was found before the mount-time syncs ran, and `_sync_native_console_chat_ui`'s card sync moved the focus the same way. Phase 9 adds no trigger and did not measurably make it more frequent: base and head show the same trigger at the same timing, and the counts are within noise (the original node, 20 interleaved serial runs: base 4 failed, head 1; with the earlier 10-run comparison, base 5 of 30, head 6 of 30). The test now waits, before it forces the focus, until focus is on the card's action, the poll has committed a credential pre-gate that is not `pending`, and no coalesced control-bar sync is scheduled (`_setup_card_settled`). It then passed 20 of 20 serial runs at 79633ad177. As a negative control in a scratch copy, it still passes 5 of 5 with only the pull-off branch removed, because `_sync_console_setup_modal`'s own deferred `focus_primary_action` makes the same move one refresh later (the test pins the transfer, not that branch, as it did before). With that deferred call removed as well, it fails 5 of 5 at its last assertion.
