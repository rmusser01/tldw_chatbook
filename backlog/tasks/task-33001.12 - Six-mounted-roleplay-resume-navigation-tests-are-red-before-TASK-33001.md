## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Rider from TASK-33001.6 (review fix round 1). TASK-33001.6 edited pins in `Tests/UI/test_console_roleplay_resume_navigation.py`, and none of them can go green locally.

**What fails.**
- Unwrapped, 9 of the 19 tests fail in setup with ADR-126 `RecoveryRequired`, because the file does not use `private_profile_test`, and a 10th fails on `_MessagePump__parent`.
- With every module-level test wrapped, 6 still fail:
  - identically at program base 144329dee0, at fb8fc1854d and at c902f34b69;
  - the per-test failure reasons at fb8fc1854d and c902f34b69 differ only by the removed 'provider-intent' event and a ContextVar address.

**Causes found.** Three tests (`test_mounted_resume_orders_consumers_once_and_suppresses_competitors`, `..._settles_first_chat_once_without_intermediate_presentation` and `..._releases_transient_first_chat_without_rollback_focus`) record a leading 'intermediate-native-sync' event.
- A stack captured at that call shows where it comes from: `ChatScreen.on_mount` → `call_after_refresh(_reconcile_console_after_attach)` → `_sync_native_console_chat_ui()`.
- This runs before the resume worker's first consumer. The attach reconcile landed in b7dd8e53f2.
- In a scratch copy, presetting `screen._console_attach_sync_complete = True` makes the ordering test pass fully, both at c902f34b69 and at fb8fc1854d.
- So the pins are otherwise right. The open question is whether the attach-time sync is an intermediate presentation that the resume contract forbids (a product fix) or whether the pins are stale (a test fix).

**Not traced.**
- `test_mounted_global_resume_outranks_registry_active_workspace`: `assert 2 == 1`.
- `test_mounted_missing_resume_falls_back_to_registry_active_workspace`: `assert 'global' == 'workspace-startup'`.
- `test_resume_navigation_continues_after_chat_handoff_release`: `LookupError` on the `active_app` ContextVar, on a bare `ChatScreen.__new__` instance.

**Fixed, then broken again on dev (Phase 9 check, 2026-10-18).** 3146bbd8da (#3011) fixed this rider: it wrapped the mounted tests in `private_profile_test` and made `_reconcile_console_after_attach` skip the attach-time sync during an explicit Resume. Phase 9's plan listed the rider as closed without code on that basis. But the closing check found five of the original reds failing again, the original way:
- `orders_consumers`, `settles_first_chat` and `releases_transient_first_chat` each get a leading 'intermediate-native-sync' event;
- `global_resume_outranks` fails `assert 2 == 1`;
- `missing_resume_falls_back` fails `'global' == 'workspace-startup'`.

The evidence:
- A serial run gave 5 failed / 14 passed at the dev merge-base 111f8e48fc. Two serial runs at the Phase 9 head fb5e86552d each gave 7 failed / 12 passed.
- A first-parent bisect on `orders_consumers` passes at 8b2120305d and fails at 14e13bb82c (Merge PR #3023, codex/console-uat-performance-fixes, 2026-10-16). That merge rewrote about 1,500 lines of chat_screen.py, including the attach reconcile. The explicit-Resume skip is still in `_reconcile_console_after_attach`, but it no longer prevents the early sync.
- The two extra failures at the Phase 9 head look intermittent. `test_mounted_resume_never_focuses_setup_modal_before_final_opener` also failed once under xdist before #3023. `test_mounted_no_resume_keeps_blocking_modal_focus_transfer`, run alone, failed 3 of 3 at the Phase 9 head and passed at 111f8e48fc. A bisect over Phase 9 landed on a commit that touches only Tests/Chat/, so it named no cause. Phase 9's base-vs-head comparison re-measures this node.

The rider stays open, and its acceptance criteria stand as written.
