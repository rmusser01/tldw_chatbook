## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Play any video the user would otherwise download: user-typed URLs and yt-dlp-supported site URLs stream through the player screen without touching disk. Every URL and redirect hop passes the SSRF egress policy; streams bypass the media byte cap by design and are time-boxed instead. ADR: backlog/decisions/044-ephemeral-generated-video-storage-playback-and-streaming.md
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 /stream-video <url> resolves direct media URLs and yt-dlp-supported site URLs with yt-dlp invoked as a subprocess only
- [ ] #2 The fully resolved final URL is validated through the egress policy before reaching ffmpeg: typed URL seeds trust and redirect hops and yt-dlp -g output are re-validated because ffmpeg follows redirects internally
- [ ] #3 ffmpeg reads streams with reconnect flags and the player handles stall and resume
- [ ] #4 Non-seekable streams disable seek with a footer note and nothing is written to disk
- [ ] #5 Stream sessions are time-boxed and user-terminated per the ADR residual-risk note
- [ ] #6 v1 scopes to progressive single-URL streams; DASH/HLS separate audio/video muxing is documented as a follow-up
