## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Library's pane frames paint $ds-column-line ($surface-lighten-2), which TASK-33003.6 did not cover (its ACs name the grid-line and control-edge boundaries). They measure 1.28:1 at origin/dev and on the Phase 3 branch, below the WCAG 1.4.11 3:1 floor for component boundaries. Found in TASK-33003.6 review round 1 (F7).
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

<!-- AC:BEGIN -->

- [x] #2 A live 211x44 capture of the Library screen records the measured ratios in the task notes.
- [x] #3 The Workflows library and navigator pane edges, which share the Library frames' line token, measure at least 3:1 against the fill they sit on, and text painted straight on that fill reads at least 4.5:1, on every shipped theme whose colours resolve. A rule test and a rendered test gate it. (Added in review round 2: the token change took those edges to 1.32:1 on litestep. Review round 3: the text of a focused button and text typed in the focused search field in those panes are held to the same 4.5:1. Review round 4: the fill of a focused control in those panes is at least as far from the pane's fill as its resting fill, and the focused search field's placeholder is held to the same 4.5:1.
