## Reading
- Committed code: MouseDown focuses the title; the guide keeps 'Focused setting: Endpoint'; the title stays at
  the offset; MouseUp + Click reach it (1 click) and the disclosure opens. The later pane_scroll_y 19 is the
  Collapsible scrolling itself into view after it opens (textual widgets/_collapsible.py:222, scroll_visible on expand).
- Early return removed: MouseDown focuses the title (no id), so handle_descendant_focus records no field and
  rewrites the guide to its no-focus copy ('Focused setting: None — Tab to a setting', ...). At the 36-column
  inspector that copy wraps 2 rows taller, so the title moves from y=23 to y=25 (scroll-off: 35 to 37) while
  pane_scroll_y does not move. MouseUp + Click land on #settings-provider-field-guide-3, the row now under
  the pointer; the title receives 0 clicks and the disclosure stays closed.
- The scroll-off variant fails the same way, so the cause is the guide reflow under the pointer, not the
  inspector's scroll-to-guide (the implementer's 'likely cause' was wrong).
- A real terminal fails the same way: App.on_event only synthesises a Click when the MouseUp widget is the
  MouseDown widget (textual 8.2.8 app.py), and here they differ. So the early return is load-bearing for
  mouse users, not only for the test; the comment at the early return now says so (fix round 1).
