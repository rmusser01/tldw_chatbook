# Phase 4 (TASK-33004) captures

These are evidence captures for the Phase 4 model-configuration PR (#2947). `task-4/` to `task-7/` hold each task's captures, taken at that task's own commit. `final/` holds re-captures taken after the cubic review of #2947, at ed975181c3 (that round's last source change).

- **Live captures**: the real app ran in tmux at 211x44 and 235x52, on a scratch profile: HOME, XDG and `TLDW_CONFIG_PATH` were scratch, with a throwaway `users_name`, a null keyring, the splash off and a fake Anthropic key. `.txt` is `tmux capture-pane -p` (text only). `.ansi.txt` is `capture-pane -p -e`, which keeps the colours. `task-6/switch-model-pick-only-pilot-*` are dumps of the real widget in a pilot app with the app stylesheets, because pick-only mode has no production opener until Phase 6.
- **Text captures do not show colour.** A highlight bar, focus and most of a scrollbar thumb are spaces. The thumb shows only its partial end cell, for example `▂▂` or `▄▄` on one row just inside the Switch model box's right edge. `final/switch-model-open-211x44.ansi.txt` shows this: the pair list's 2-column scrollbar holds the thumb on rows 7–28, its partial end on row 29 and the track on rows 30–34. The 25-model `task-6` open and down captures show the thumb's bottom edge, then its top edge, on different rows. `task-6/chat-settings-model-list-down-*` is after one Down press. That list fits without scrolling, so only the highlight bar moves (onto `● CURRENT`), and `chat-settings-model-down-highlight-rows.txt` records it row by row.
