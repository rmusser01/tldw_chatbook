Reading: 'PUMP:<widget>' = the current asyncio task is that widget's message-pump task and the widget sits
below a removed root; every such row never landed. 'ctx_pump' is textual._context.active_message_pump: it
reads 'Canvas' on two rows that COMPLETED (call_after_refresh runs on the screen's task), which is why the
guard compares tasks and not that variable. The two WORKER rows are a different failure (the worker is
cancelled mid-swap when its owner unmounts) and are NOT covered by the guard.
