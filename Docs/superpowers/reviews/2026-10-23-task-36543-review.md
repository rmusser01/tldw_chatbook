## Result

Self-reviewed the pure PCM/filter/snapshot contracts before starting device-output
integration. No unresolved blocking finding remains. This unit neither opens a
device nor drives a Console portrait by itself.

`PcmEnvelope` handles little-endian PCM16, incomplete interleaved channel frames,
partial final windows and rational sample-rate boundaries. Energy uses all channel
samples, so stereo phase cancellation does not become silence. Retained producer
state consists of one partial frame, scalar energy and source counters; accepted
chunks alone may enter the analyzer. No provider/device/UI dependency is imported.

`SpeechEnvelopeFilter` gates before smoothing. Digital zero/invalid energy resets
immediately, two below-threshold windows close, and reopening seeds a finite value.
Smoothing uses actual window duration with the approved attack/release/hysteresis.

`SpeechPresentation` retains one latest immutable snapshot for its exact read-only
issued owner. It rejects old generation/timestamp publication, preserves confirmed
pause and terminal state, and degrades stale, future or invalid timing to closed.
A quiet closed gate clears the meter level while retaining qualified device timing.
Clock failure cannot escape into audio callers. There is no reader callback, queue,
thread or timer, and no audio history is persisted.
