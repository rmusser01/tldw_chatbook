## Purpose and evidence rules

This is the before/after capability ledger for the staged Library adaptive-reader migrations. It
records visible behavior before each destination's production edit. A migration may move a control
into an approved region, but it may not drop or change the authority of a capability without
updating the approved design.

The **Before status** column describes only behavior observable in the current implementation.
`Present` does not mean that every referenced baseline test is green in this worktree; it means the
control and handler exist and the cited tests are the current preservation evidence. `Approved
addition` marks behavior that ADR-086 and the approved design require but the current Conversations
canvas does not provide. The **After / evidence** cells intentionally remain placeholders until the
production migration is implemented and verified.

Approved regions use the three structural roles from ADR-086:

- **Library**: the shared destination rail.
- **Destination list**: the destination-owned list, including query, paging, bulk, and list-level
  recovery.
- **Work pane**: the permanent destination-owned reader, editor, or task surface.

The structural shell owns geometry, grips, focus-region integration, and late-bound slots only.
`LibraryScreen`, destination state, and the current services remain the capability authorities.
The row-level **After / evidence** cells below are the preregistered proof obligations; the
post-change verification ledger following both tables records the final result and evidence for
those obligations without rewriting the baseline.
