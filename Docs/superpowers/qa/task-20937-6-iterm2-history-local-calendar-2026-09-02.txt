## Reproduction and correction

The pre-fix History view grouped conversations labeled `4h` beneath
`YESTERDAY` even though iTerm2 and the host calendar were still on September 2.
The loader defaulted a missing `app_instance.local_timezone_name` to UTC; that
attribute is not populated by the application, so UTC was the effective
production default.

The fix keeps a valid explicit IANA timezone authoritative. With no valid
explicit timezone, `datetime.astimezone(None)` delegates each date conversion
to the host's local rules. This avoids freezing the current UTC offset and
therefore preserves DST-aware calendar grouping without a new dependency.

The same isolated database and iTerm2 window were restarted from fixed source.
At 235x52, History then placed the five-hour-old conversations beneath `TODAY`;
the recent Roleplay item also appeared beneath `TODAY` with its current
minute-level age. A `tmux capture-pane` of the same frame independently
reported `235x52`, `TODAY`, and the `5h` rows.
