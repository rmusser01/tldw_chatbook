### Important

1. **Required-result validation must start from attempts and workflow state, not only surviving published reservations.** `tldw_chatbook/DB/recovery_workflow_schema.py:189` only checks consistency of rows already present in `workflow_payload_reservations`; `tldw_chatbook/DB/workflow_runs.py:158` has the same gap in snapshots. A terminal attempt can therefore lose both its reservation and result, or have its result deleted and reservation marked `removed` while the workflow is still active/undrained, without an explicit missing-evidence failure. The validator treats the latter as a valid tombstone even though `remove_payloads` (`workflow_runs.py:509`) would refuse that removal through the supported API. This lets malformed archive state bypass the retention invariant that this task promises to qualify.

   **Reproduction:** opened the retained `task-1-native/capture-agent-runs.db` with SQLite `mode=ro&immutable=1`, copied it into separate in-memory databases, and changed only those copies. The original workflow/attempt state was `('awaiting_approval', 'failed', 'pending')`. Original validation returned `()`. Deleting `workflow_results` and `workflow_payload_reservations` also returned `()`. Separately, deleting `workflow_results` and updating reservations to `state='removed'` returned `()`. All three copies had an empty `PRAGMA foreign_key_check` result. No catalog mutation or disabled foreign keys were needed.

   **Fix:** enforce the legal attempt/result/reservation/workflow-state combinations in both history reads and recovery validation. At minimum, terminal result-bearing attempts must retain their required reservation and payload unless a legitimate settled workflow removal tombstone applies; a removed reservation cannot excuse missing evidence on an active or undrained workflow. Preserve legitimate prepared/no-result and intentionally removed settled histories. Add focused tests for both reproduced cases; the existing missing-result test (`Tests/DB/test_workflow_runs.py:316`) covers only a surviving `published` reservation.
