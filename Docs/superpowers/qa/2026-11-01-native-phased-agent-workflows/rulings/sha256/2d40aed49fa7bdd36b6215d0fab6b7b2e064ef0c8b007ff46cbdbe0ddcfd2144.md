### I1 — A replaced source is rejected only after its native engine has been published

**Locations:** `tldw_chatbook/Chat/console_runtime.py:3687`, `:3776`, `:3798`; connected publication lock at `:3539`; post-publication check at `tldw_chatbook/Chat/console_plugin_hook_preparation.py:103`.

After `plugin_request.check(service)` succeeds, `ensure_hooks_v2()` acquires `_run_hooks_lock`. If the original capacity/source is replaced while that acquisition is waiting, the method still stores the HookEngine, the caller sets `engine.native_plugins`, and `_hooks_v2_configured` records its signature. Only then does the preparation context detect replacement and raise. The failed preparation therefore leaves a configured native engine available for reuse rather than conclusively discarding the stale result.

**Reproduction:** The review probe installed a real trusted selected Stop-hook package through the real PluginService/SQLite fixture. A second thread held the original runtime lock, observed the host thread blocked in actual `ensure_hooks_v2` at line 3539, replaced `_execution_capacity`, and released the lock. The original capacity still held one tool worker at the witnessed wait. The awaited preparation raised `RuntimeError("Original plugin hook preparation changed.")`, but `get_hooks_v2(session_id)` returned an engine whose `native_plugins.service` was the original service. No hook command was executed, and no later-event permission bypass is claimed.

**Evidence:** `task-8d2b2a-review-probe-configured.log:211` records `actual_lock_wait: 3539`, `tool_workers_while_waiting: 1`, `stale_engine_published: True`, and `native_service_published: True`. The desired no-publication assertion fails at `:291`; final result at `:302`: **1 failed in 8.07s**. The retained probe is `task-8d2b2a-review-probe.py` (also `/private/tmp/test_task8d2b2a_publication_probe.py`).

**Smallest complete fix:** Put a concrete memory-only original-source fence at the actual engine/native/signature publication boundary after acquiring the relevant lock. Keep external activation/permission/FS reads outside bookkeeping locks, and preserve the same finite worker/host/release custody. A rejected preparation must not leave its newly prepared native engine installed. Promote this real lock-contention case alongside the existing awaited-host-read tests.
