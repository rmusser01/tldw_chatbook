## Independent review fix round 1 — required-history retention

Base: `cfde251667a23a1e5ac1dcfde8b3d43d14b8b391`. Reproduced Important finding 1 without catalog changes, foreign-key disabling, fixture/bootstrap changes or archive/source rewrites. The old checks started from surviving reservations, so deleted reservation/result pairs and invalid removed tombstones escaped validation.

The fix starts from every task attempt and shares one passive `_required_results` check between history snapshots and recovery validation. Completed/failed/uncertain and native-run-bearing closed attempts require their owned reservation plus published payload unless the workflow has a legitimate removed tombstone. Removed histories must have no active/undrained task/phase/interval or uncertain automatic chain/reservation; required tombstones cannot be absent/reserved and cannot retain results. Active histories cannot claim removed reservations. Prepared attempts with no results remain valid with or without capacity reserved. Accepted attempts require reservation; published outcomes must agree with the result-bearing attempt (closed history may retain its original typed result). This adds no runtime owner, dispatch, schema/migration/catalog change, permission or authority.
